# LEADS — Grudge Mixes + Insane Music

**The single register of every track, recording and lead in this repository.** Rebuilt 2026-11-20 from all dossiers (`projects/grudge-mixes/`, `projects/insane-music/`), the Drive inventories, the legacy `sloev/insanemusic` repo and the discovery passes of 2026-11-20. The planned mixes are in [MIXES.md](MIXES.md). How to keep this file current is in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).

## How to read an entry

- **Project:** Grudge Mixes = recorded grievance-response, before 1926 (`projects/grudge-mixes/METHODOLOGY.md`). Insane Music / *Music in Spite* = music made where it was not expected or tolerated: confinement, banned or unwelcome voices, DIY and found tapes; any date (`projects/insane-music/method.md`). "Both" = fits both rule sets.
- **Research status:** copied from the dossier. Grudge Mixes grades A/B/C; Insane Music VERIFIED / PARTIALLY VERIFIED / LEAD plus a visibility rating. Nothing in this repository is cleared for release.
- **Downloaded:** "Yes (listed)" = the file name is in the project Google Drive per the owner's listing of 2026-11-17 or a logged Grok deposit; nobody has yet played, timed or checksummed it. "No" = not in Drive.
- **Sources, listen, download or buy:** every URL in the dossier, labelled where the site type is known (Internet Archive and LOC Jukebox usually allow download; DAHR, FAU, Bandcamp, Phonobase stream or sell). A streaming page is not permission to reuse.
- **Dossier:** the file that holds the evidence. If this register and a dossier disagree, the dossier wins until this file is fixed.

#### GM-UK-001 — Candidate: “Revenge! Timotheus Cries” — Handel, *Alexander's Feast* (Vocalion X-9560, 1924/25)

- **Project:** Grudge Mixes · **Research status:** see dossier · **Planned mix:** Vol. 2 The Debt
- **Downloaded:** No
- **Recording:**
    - **Performer:** Malcolm McEachern, bass; Aeolian Orchestra.
    - **Label/catalogue:** Vocalion X-9560, 10-inch 78rpm, issued in the United Kingdom.
    - **Session/matrix:** specialist Vocalion sessionography lists London, **October 1924?**, matrix/session numbers **03724** and **03725**, with “Revenge Timotheus cries” parts 1 and 3 and “Behold a ghastly band” part 2; issue X-9560 is linked to 03724 and …
    - **Publication:** the UK Vocalion catalogue's **1926 edition** (covering issues through November 1925) lists X-9560 as “Revenge! Timotheus Cries,” *Alexander's Feast* (Handel), parts I and II, with Aeolian Orchestra, bass Malcolm McEachern; its foreword says …
    - **Cross-check:** a later McEachern discography independently lists X-9560 and matrices/issues 03724/03725. A 2010 specialist review says McEachern recorded the aria on Vocalion in the early 1920s and comments on the 1924 studio circumstances; useful …
    - No exact item-level public archive audio or preservation-transfer record has yet been located. Do not claim that the audio has been heard or that the two parts are complete without inspecting the physical issue.
- **Background:** This is a particularly strong example of music represented as an instrument of retaliation. In Handel's *Alexander's Feast*, the singer Timotheus evokes the Furies and the ghosts of Greeks slain in earlier wars with Persia. His song moves Alexander toward burning Persepolis, framed by the poem as vengeance for Greek dead. The aria is not simply an aggressive title: the libretto explicitly stages a remembered injury, summoned dead, and a retaliatory act. It is a fictionalized ancient-historical narrative mediated through John Dryden's 1697 ode and Newburgh Hamilton's adaptation, not a neutral record of Alexander's actual motives.
