In 1990, Deaf activist and broadcaster Paddy Ladd helped make a funk video with a group of Deaf performers. Researcher Joana Pereira identifies it as the first signed-song video written and performed by Deaf people. Ladd wrote the song, first titled “You Can Lie Through Your Teeth (But You Can’t Lie Through Your Body)”; it was later retitled “No Body No Lie”. Clive Mason, Colin Thompson, Clara Allerdyce and Charles Hampton performed it.

That title is a whole argument in seven words. The video belongs to a moment when Deaf artists were creating pop in their own language and on their own terms, rather than appearing as an afterthought in somebody else’s performance. Ladd had already been making Deaf television in Britain and later helped bring signed music to major concerts and festivals. This is a recording we want to find, not a claim that it is currently easy to watch.
