Ramūnas Jaras formed Echidna Aukštyn in Lithuania and issued *Paskutinė požemių muzika* (The Last Music of the Dungeons) on cassette in 1993. The recording is murky and homemade: low frequencies, lo-fi crackle, basement-piano tones and voices thrown into echo. It sounds like music leaking through a wall from a room that might not officially exist.

Jaras had already made tiny-run experimental tapes under his own name in 1987 and 1988. Echidna Aukštyn became one of his more recognisable projects, moving between rock, indie pop and experimental music. A 1994 cassette by the group later appeared on the commercial pop label Pūkas, an odd landing place for such an underground sound. This first cassette is the rougher document: a DIY release made before the project found even that unlikely route into the shops.
