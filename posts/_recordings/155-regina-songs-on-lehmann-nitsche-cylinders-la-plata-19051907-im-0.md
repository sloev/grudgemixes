Regina, a Mapuche captive of the "Conquest of the Desert", lived as a servant in the household of the scientist who recorded her. Hers are among the first recordings of Mapuche song.

**Recording:** Berliner Phonogramm-Archiv, "Araukaner" cylinders, VII WS 192; cylinder 2 on BPhA-WA4/5 (2009); audio with Canio Llanquinao & Pozo Menares, *Revista Musical Chilena* 68 (2014)

**Why it's here:** song recorded by the people who took her land; the voice of the only woman in the corpus.

**Consent:** ⚠ sacred *machi* songs recorded under coercion. Mapuche community consent is required before any use.
