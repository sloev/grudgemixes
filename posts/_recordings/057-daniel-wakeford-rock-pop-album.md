Daniel Wakeford has been writing and recording songs since 2009. *The Rock-Pop Album* arrived on 1 December 2022, his birthday, credited to the Daniel Wakeford Experience. Wakeford sings lead; the band brings in keyboards, violin, viola, cello, guitars, bass, drums and Moog. The songs were recorded and mixed at Brighton Road Recording Studios.

Wakeford identifies as autistic and is part of Brighton’s learning-disabled music scene. His catalogue already ran to dozens of songs before this album, and the record treats that songwriting as the main event. There are big choruses and direct declarations, but no need to file it as a novelty. A title like “I’m A Rock-Popstar” is not a request for permission. It is the job description.
