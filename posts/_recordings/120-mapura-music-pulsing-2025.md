Māpura Studios in Tāmaki Makaurau Auckland runs weekly creative music sessions built around spontaneous music-making. *Pulsing* gathers live recordings from the final term of 2025. Its six tracks include “Australian”, which runs nearly eleven minutes, “Yung Man Blues”, “Respect” and “Teresa’s Pulse”. The album credits twenty performers, plus facilitator and musician Stefan Neville.

The studio’s approach is less about teaching a room to reproduce a fixed idea than making space for people to discover what happens when they play together. That matters in a music scene where disabled artists are too often offered a simplified version of somebody else’s repertoire. Here the material is credited to the people in the room, and the room itself is part of the sound. *Pulsing* is a document of collective invention, with no single frontperson claiming the whole current.
