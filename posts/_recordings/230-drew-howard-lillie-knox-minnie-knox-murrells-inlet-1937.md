The Library of Congress finding aid identifies one disc, AFS 903, containing songs and hymns sung by Drew Howard, Lillie Knox and Minnie Knox. It was recorded at Murrells Inlet, South Carolina, in January 1937. The catalogue gives no commercial release details or wider biography for the singers, so this entry stays close to what the archive can establish: three named local voices, one disc, and a specific place and date. That modest record is the point. It preserves a community performance outside the commercial music industry and gives us names that might otherwise have disappeared from the recorded history of the region.
