On 11 September 1914, Victor’s engineers recorded Henry Julian in Port of Spain. The label named him “J. Resigna”; audiences knew him as Julian Whiterose, leader of the White Rose Masquerade Band. “Iron Duke in the Land” is one of the earliest surviving vocal calypsos and is often cited as the first English-language calypso on record. Julian uses the chantwell’s voice to announce his own standing in the band, taking the title “Iron Duke” as a public persona. This is not a studio imitation of a folk tradition: it is a Carnival singer carrying a local performance form onto a commercial 78-rpm disc.
