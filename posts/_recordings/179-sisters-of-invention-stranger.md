The Sisters of Invention formed in South Australia in 2010. Aimee Crathern, Michelle Hall, Caroline Hardy and Annika Hooper make pop as a group of learning-disabled artists supported by Tutti Arts. Their second album, *Stranger*, arrived in 2022, seven years after their self-titled debut.

The group’s work is not a workshop exercise dressed up as a record. The members write and perform, and have worked with collaborators from the Australian music scene to develop their sound. In a 2026 Bandcamp Daily feature, vocalist Michelle Hall described the practical business of making a record: waiting for a vocal take, adjusting lyrics with the others, and getting the song right. That is the work of a band. *Stranger* is their album, not somebody else’s idea of what their music should be.
