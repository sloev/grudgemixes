In the 1970s, three Deaf college students gathered in a Gallaudet University dorm room to play rock music and imagine a future as a band. They called themselves Beethoven’s Nightmare. They kept going for decades, playing venues around the United States and overseas, before releasing their first CD, *Turn It Up Louder*, in Hawaii in May 2005.

The Honolulu *Star-Bulletin* reported that the album was believed to be the first known CD by an all-Deaf rock band. Bassist Ed Chevy described the release as the payoff after more than thirty years. The title is an excellent answer to anyone who thought the band was a novelty: the music had been there all along; the record industry was late to the party. Their own site still calls them a Deaf rock-and-roll band with more than fifty years of performance history.
