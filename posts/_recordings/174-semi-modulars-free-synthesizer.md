The Semi-Modulars began in a synthesis and sound-design class at the Texas School for the Blind and Visually Impaired. Teacher Dan Butler noticed that analog synthesizers could be explored by touch: unlike many digital instruments, their knobs and switches do not hide behind menus. The students turned the class into a band and kept making records.

*Free Synthesizer*, released in April 2026, contains eleven pieces written and performed by the students. The titles tell you something about the fun they are having: “Cheesy Italian-American Spaceship”, “Jalapeno Tornado” and “The Haphazard Ghosts of TSBVI”. The album was recorded at the school between August 2025 and February 2026. No teacher’s demonstration tape: these are the students’ compositions, played by the people who made them.
