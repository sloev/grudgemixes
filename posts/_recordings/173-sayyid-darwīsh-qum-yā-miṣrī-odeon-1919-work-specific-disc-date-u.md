The National Phonotheque of Tunisia catalogs an Odeon disc titled “Qum yā Miṣrī” under Sayyid Darwish’s name as item D-5239 and identifies Darwish as its composer. The item record leaves the specific recording date blank; the wider Darwish collection covers 1919–1932. The song is associated with Egypt’s 1919 uprising and became part of the language of collective resistance. Darwish himself died in 1923, but the surviving disc record does not establish who performed this particular issue. This entry keeps that distinction visible and gathers the work under one artist record rather than creating a separate post for every title.
