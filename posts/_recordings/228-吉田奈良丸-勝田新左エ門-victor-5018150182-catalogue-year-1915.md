These two Victor catalogue entries preserve Yoshida Naramaru’s rōkyoku recitations of the Akō vendetta cycle: “Katsuta Shinzaemon” and “Yazū Uemon no Seichū.” The 1915 Victor catalogue lists four parts of each title under Yoshida Naramaru. The official Kishiwada City Library identifies the performer as the second-generation storyteller, who took the title at 23, made his first record in 1910, relinquished the Naramaru name in 1929, and continued as Yamato no Jō until his death in 1967. This entry keeps both recitations together under one artist. Rōkyoku remains underrepresented in Western record histories, although Naramaru himself was a major performer in Japan; the point here is the overlooked form, not an unsupported claim that the artist was unknown.
