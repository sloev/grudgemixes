Rudely Interrupted have spent years making bright, tightly arranged pop-rock while insisting that disabled musicians belong in the middle of the music business, not in a separate novelty corner. Their third album, *Emergency*, appeared on Cheersquad Records & Tapes in January 2025.

The title had more than one meaning. The band’s founding member and co-writer Rohan Brooks suffered a brain aneurysm in 2018, spent a year in hospital and was left with severe memory loss. The group eventually returned with a record that includes a climate-warning title track, “Paying It Back” and “Alcohol Soul”. The latter turns a life story into a sharp little pop narrative. The album is not a comeback slogan; it is the sound of a band continuing to write, arrange and release its own songs.
