Rivky Grossman taught herself music. On *Angel Sings the Blues*, released in 2013, she sings, plays piano and writes the songs; Brian Sanders plays cello, while Bob Brockmann and Amy Lee share production credits. The result moves between folk, blues, cabaret and pop, with the influence of Grossman’s Hasidic upbringing audible in the musical mix.

Berklee’s ABLE Voices programme interviewed Grossman as a disabled artist. She spoke about living with schizo-affective disorder in her own terms, describing it as part of the background of daily life and of her creative experience. That context belongs here because Grossman chose to speak about it, not because a diagnosis explains the songs. The record itself is the thing: a self-made set of songs with an unusual beat, a piano and a voice that knows its own way around a chorus.
