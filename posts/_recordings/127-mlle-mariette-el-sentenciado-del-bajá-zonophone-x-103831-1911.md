The Hebrew University archive identifies this as a shortened version of the Sephardic romance “El sentenciado del bajá.” In longer tellings, a protagonist—female in some versions, male in others—is sentenced to death after refusing the advances of an unnamed “Turk.” The story then turns to a dialogue with the protagonist’s mother, who is asked to intervene but cannot help; that exchange is missing from this record. Mlle Mariette is one of two women in the EMI collection identified only by first name, each represented by a single song. Her 1911 Zonophone preserves a rare female voice in the Ladino commercial repertoire.
