On 8 May 1938, John D. Weaver of Landrum, South Carolina, played the fiddle tune “The Fox Chase” at the National Folk Festival in Washington, D.C. The Library of Congress finding aid identifies the performance as AFS 9833A3, one disc within a larger group of festival recordings made by the U.S. Recording Company. The entry offers no commercial issue or substantial biography for Weaver. What remains is a precise trace of a local fiddler and a tune carried from the South Carolina foothills to a national folk gathering. It belongs here as a small, under-documented performance preserved by an archive rather than the record business.
