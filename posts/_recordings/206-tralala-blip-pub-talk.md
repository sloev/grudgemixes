Lydian Dunbar wrote “Pub Talk” after a night when staff at his local pub did not understand his speech and, as he told ABC presenter Eliza Hull, did not talk to him much. Dunbar has Down syndrome. Rather than turn the experience into somebody else’s lesson, he brought it into the studio and asked his bandmate Randy Reimann to make a song with him.

They had just got their hands on an 808 drum machine. The track grew through the band’s electronic setup, with Dunbar involved in the recording process as well as supplying the story. “Pub Talk” appeared on Tralala Blip’s 2019 album *Eat My Codes If Your Light Falls*. It is a small, exact account of being talked past, sent back into the room at dance-floor volume.
