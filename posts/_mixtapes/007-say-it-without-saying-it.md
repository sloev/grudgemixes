A mixtape about what happens when someone in power decides a song is dangerous.

It opens in Cairo in 1919, where a fruit-seller's cry carried a banned man's name. Then come a samba that teased the police chief and a singer's forbidden name carried on a banned song. Next comes music bootlegged onto hospital X-rays, a wartime cantata that survived on tape, and a Baltic punk recording passed hand to hand.

Each time the music changes carrier, code or country. It does not go away.
