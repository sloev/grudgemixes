Gaelynn Lea composed the music for Sam Gold’s 2022 Broadway production of *Macbeth*. In 2025 she released the music as she had originally imagined it, before the practical edits and omissions required by live theatre. *Music from Macbeth* is structured as five long instrumental movements, one for each act, with the vocal songs “Starving” and “Perfect” at either end.

Lea plays violin and sings, joined by Jeremy Ylvisaker on guitar and bass, Dave Mehling on keyboards and piano, and Al Church on drums and percussion. Lea was born with osteogenesis imperfecta and developed an upright violin technique that works with her body. She is also a disability-rights advocate. The record is not a demonstration of adaptation; it is a substantial, darkly shaped piece of theatre music that has found a life beyond the stage.
