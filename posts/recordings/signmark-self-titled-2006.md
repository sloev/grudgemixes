Marko Vuoriheimo, who performs as Signmark, released his self-titled debut in 2006. It was an eight-track package: a CD soundtrack and a DVD of signed music videos. Vuoriheimo was born Deaf and performs in Finnish Sign Language, using rhythm, handshape and facial expression to build his rhymes. Hearing collaborators provide the spoken and rapped vocals alongside the signed performance.

The songs are not just party tracks with a new visual trick. “Carl Oscar Malm” pays tribute to the founder of Finland’s first school for Deaf pupils, and other songs take up Deaf history, rights and everyday life. The album made a claim about who gets to be a rapper and what a rap performance can look like. Signmark later signed with Warner, but the first record is the more interesting artefact: a small, self-titled declaration that a language built in the hands can carry the beat.
