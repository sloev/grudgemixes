A self-released cassette attributed to the mysterious Dutch project Hora Est is one of those home-taping finds that seems to have been made in a room with too many keyboards and no reason to stop. The tape moves between electronic experiments, bedroom pop, cheap-keyboard minimalism and ambient drone. Its track list includes “Nasty Young One”, “Wonder Baby”, “Gymnastics”, “Casiobility” and “Violinen”.

The maker’s identity remains unclear. A full-tape transfer circulated through the Archaic Inventions channel after the recording had been brought back to attention by the No Longer Forgotten Music blog. That uncertainty matters: the cassette is an artefact of a home-recording culture in which names, sleeves and distribution could disappear almost as quickly as the music was made. The tape survives; the biography does not.
