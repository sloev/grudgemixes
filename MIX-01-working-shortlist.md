**Reserve / contrast slots (not counted):**

## Balance problems the editor should see

- **Five of six slots are Euro-American opera or vaudeville.** The README's global mandate asks for better than that. Slot 6 (Japan) is the most advanced non-European item. Mexico (Parra), the Armenian diaspora (slot 1 partly counts) and West Africa (Zonophone 1925 cluster) are the next routes, but each is C-level on content.
- **Three slots are operatic revenge arias** (2, 3, 4). Consider keeping two. On evidence alone, Hagen and Sì vendetta are the strongest.
- **Two of the strongest items are in English translation** (2, 3). That is historically true to the market, but sequence them apart.
