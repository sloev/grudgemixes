## Current methodology
- Treat this repository as an international historical recorded-sound research compendium, not a conventional software application. “Module-by-module” therefore means auditing each repository area and its information architecture: root documentation, candidate records, research dossiers/maps, and cooperation/decision logs.
- Core recording cutoff: fixed or recorded before 1926-01-01. Distinguish VERIFIED, PROBABLE, LEAD, REJECTED (or the repository's current equivalent); separate recording identity, date, thematic relevance, audio access/authenticity, and rights status.
- Expanded thematic scope: grievance, retaliation, revenge, punishment, defiance/resistance, accusation/protest and feuds. Lament/betrayal/resentment qualifies only when the song contains an accusation, demand, threat, vow, response or retaliatory intention. Generic sadness/anger is insufficient.
- Do not use author-death as a discovery gate. Require evidence for chronology, provenance, relevant text and recording match; unknowns remain explicit.
- Search from local song forms/narratives and original-language text toward dated printed witnesses, catalogues, matrices, labels and audio. Preserve language/script, local terminology, variant readings, translation uncertainty and colonial/archive context.
- Distinguish primary evidence from secondary scholarship and catalogue metadata. A tradition's existence does not prove that a specific commercial recording belongs to it. No candidate promotion from title alone.
- Rights clearance and audio-transfer authenticity are separate from historical/thematic eligibility.
- Maintain citations and exact source URLs in dossiers. Record negative searches and access limitations rather than turning absence of evidence into rejection.
- User's established editorial governance: Claude Fable has final editorial authority; ChatGPT/Grok contribute research and drafts.
