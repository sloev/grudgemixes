Kept by Accident

▶ 8 of 8 playable
mixtape8 tracks1980s1990s2010s

Most music disappears, and the eight recordings on this mixtape very nearly did. A Ghanaian rap tape pressed in fifty copies, a Gnawa cassette with a drummer nobody can name, forty grimy tapes from behind a butcher's counter in Queens, a reporter's cassettes from a Cape Town cellar, single-copy home tapes from Vilnius and a whispering Lithuanian tape almost nobody heard. Add a Japanese label that released exactly 222 cassettes and a stranger who paid to have her poem made into a record. Each survived by luck: found in markets, cellars, shop bins and archives.

Say It Without Saying It

▶ 5 of 8 playable
mixtape8 tracks1910s1940s1950s1980s2010s

Eight songs that authorities banned, erased or punished, and how they got through anyway. A Cairo singer sells dates so a whole city can sing a forbidden name. A samba jokes about the police that harassed it. Banned jazz and rock'n'roll are cut onto hospital X-rays. Algeria's raï grandmother keeps singing after the state shuts her out, Latvian and Estonian bands outlive their bans on tape, a cantata from wartime Riga waits on tape for its return in 1990, and a London drill duo is sentenced for performing a song.
