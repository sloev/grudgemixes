Erik Satie left behind a single page of slow, unresolved music headed by a note about playing it 840 times in a row. He never published it and never played it.

On 9–10 September 1963 John Cage took the note at its word. At the Pocket Theatre in Manhattan a relay of pianists, among them John Cale, played it for 18 hours and 40 minutes. Ticket holders got a refund of a nickel for every 20 minutes they stayed.
