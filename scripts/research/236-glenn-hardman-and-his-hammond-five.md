Glenn Hardman and His Hammond Five made six sides in Chicago on 26 June 1939, then effectively vanished. The lineup was an unlikely little collision: Hardman on electric organ, Lester Young on tenor saxophone and clarinet, Lee Castle on trumpet, Freddie Green on guitar and Jo Jones on drums.

“China Boy” was issued on Columbia 35341. The other titles included “Exactly Like You”, “On the Sunny Side of the Street” and Hardman’s own “Upright Organ Blues”. The session is an odd early glimpse of the Hammond in jazz, before the instrument acquired its familiar role. Hardman’s organ is a strange, forward colour against the relaxed swing around it; Lester Young seems to float above the machine.

The famous names in the room have kept this session alive. Its leader remains a near-ghost.
