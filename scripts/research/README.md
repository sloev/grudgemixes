## Editorial cautions

- Schalkenbach's original 1862 *Piano-Orchestre* was non-electrical; electrical controls and remote effects were added to later versions.
- The Choralcelo's surviving audio is a damaged original glass-master transfer, not a modern reconstruction. Sources disagree about the tune title and one mirror gives a different year.
- The Staccatone, Sphäraphon, and Alfred Graham's feedback apparatus are documented by contemporary accounts, but no eligible original recordings have been located.
- Kastner's pyrophone makes acoustic tones from vibrating flames. It is a remarkable electromechanical-adjacent instrument, not an electronic synthesizer.
- Do not turn a historical research note into a public recording post without a recording that satisfies the repository's access and date rules.
