Malinda Jackson Parker was a former member of Liberia's House of Representatives who sat at the piano and half-sang, half-spoke her own songs: odes, praise songs, and a song about a mosquito. *The Liberian Landmark Joy* came out around 1971 and has been rediscovered abroad as "outsider music".

We would rather hear how she was heard at home: as a singular, self-made voice, and a woman's, in a style nobody else used.
