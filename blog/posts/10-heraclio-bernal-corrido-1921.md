Heraclio Bernal was a Sinaloa bandit-rebel betrayed for a reward in 1888. The corrido that followed made sure the betrayal was remembered as clearly as the man.

Trío Nava recorded "Heraclio Bernal" for Columbia in 1921. It is a ballad doing a newspaper's and a court's job at once: who did it, who paid, who sold whom. A transfer survives in UCLA's Frontera Collection of Mexican and Mexican-American recordings.
