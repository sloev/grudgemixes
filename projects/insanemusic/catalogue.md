# Catalogue: consolidated register

**Consolidated 2026-11-15 from `coop.log.md` and `environment-leads.md`.** Until now the project's findings were spread across about 30 log entries and a 450-line leads file, with overlapping numbering (two sections numbered 14 and two numbered 15) and no stable IDs. This file gives every recording-level item one **IM-ID**, one status and one visibility rating, and points back to the detailed evidence. The detailed files remain the evidence of record; this is the index.

Status vocabulary follows [method.md](method.md) §5. Visibility ratings follow §3B. **“Verified” means existence and provenance are documented by sources; it does not mean anyone on the project has listened to the recording.** Where this consolidation pass re-checked a primary source, the *Checked* column says so.

## E. Coverage gaps (state them; do not hide them)

- **Searching has been almost entirely in English.** No systematic searches yet in Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Russian, Arabic, Persian, Hindi/Urdu, Bengali, Chinese, Japanese or Korean, beyond the Greek and Philippine items found through English sources.
- **The psychiatric-institution core is thin:** four entries (IM-001 to IM-004), two of them famous (IM-003, IM-004). Kalaupapa, Carville and Uzuakoli are Hansen's-disease settings, not psychiatric ones, and are tagged G. Most strong finds are prisons, camps or Hansen's-disease settlements. That is a real result of environment-first searching, but if the title stays *Insane Music* the editor should know the asylum core is the least developed part.
- **Regions with no entries:** Latin America, the Middle East, South Asia, mainland East Asia, Eastern Europe and the former USSR, and Oceania except Hawai‘i and Melbourne.
- **Nothing has been listened to** apart from the confirmation that the IM-008 files exist.
