## Template (adapt per row; English, short, no assumptions about the people recorded)

> Subject: Research enquiry about [identifier]
>
> Dear [name],
>
> I am researching a non-commercial discography of overlooked recordings made in institutions and closed communities. [Item] ([identifiers]) appears in your [collection / page / article]. Could you tell me [one to three specific questions from the table]?
>
> We do not republish audio without permission, we follow your access and credit conditions, and we do not identify performers beyond what they or the archive have agreed to.
>
> With thanks,
> [owner's name and project contact]

## After a reply

Record the outcome in `coop.log.md` (the date, who replied and what they said, without private contact details) and update the row's status in [catalogue.md](catalogue.md). Note permission conditions word for word.
