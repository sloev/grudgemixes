# `Тайна третьей планеты` / The Mystery of the Third Planet (1981)

## Identification

- Original title: `Тайна третьей планеты`
- Transliteration: `Tayna tretyey planety` (also `Tayna tret'ey planety`)
- English titles: `The Mystery of the Third Planet`; sometimes `The Secret of the Third Planet`
- Year: 1981
- Director: Roman Kachanov (Роман Качанов)
- Studio: Soyuzmultfilm (Союзмультфильм)
- Screenplay/story: Kir Bulychev (Кир Булычёв), based on `Путешествие Алисы` / `Alice's Journey`
- Music credit: Aleksandr Zatsepin (Александр Зацепин)

## Music credit

**Evidence status: WELL-SUPPORTED**

Film Festival Ghent's programme entry credits original music to Aleksandr Zatsepin. The Russian Animation in Letters and Figures database also lists “music Alexander Zatsepin” in its production credits.

## Electronic instruments and studio practice

**Evidence status: WELL-SUPPORTED for named instruments and DIY devices; exact film-cue mapping still open**

Multiple independent Russian-language sources describe Zatsepin’s electronic approach on this score:

- A Kinopoisk feature on Zatsepin’s centenary states that for `Тайна третьей планеты` and the TV film `31 июня` he fully pursued electronic instruments, and that he designed an instrument called **оркестрола** — an early sampler-like device using tape recordings of orchestral instruments and voices.
- The same article and later concert coverage name **MiniMoog** among the commercial instruments used.
- In 2022 interview/coverage around a live reconstruction concert, Zatsepin described a four-octave keyboard instrument on which he recorded vocal ranges (including Tanya Antsiferova) and then played those voices as playable samples — effectively a homemade tape/voice instrument distinct from the commercial Minimoog.
- Coverage of the 2021–2022 reconstruction project states that the **original soundtrack master is lost**, that the team worked from the original score (partitura) plus a film mix with effects but without dialogue, and that synthesizer timbres were reconstructed rather than taken from surviving session tapes.

Sources:
- [Kinopoisk, «100 лет композитору Александру Зацепину…»](https://www.kinopoisk.ru/media/article/4012441/) — оркестрола, MiniMoog, electronic focus on this film.
- [Smotrim, concert report 17 May 2022](https://smotrim.ru/journal/2747215) and [follow-up](https://smotrim.ru/journal/2747855) — Zatsepin on lost original instruments, vocal sampling keyboard, reconstruction method.
- Reconstruction project statements summarised in secondary coverage: original master lost; score + film stem used; 2021 Mosfilm studio sessions under Zatsepin’s supervision.

**Caution:** These sources establish named instruments and a DIY sampling practice. They do not yet supply a cue-by-cue instrument map or surviving original session audio. Treat reconstructed releases as reconstructions, not as the 1981 master.

## Soundtrack releases

**Evidence status: WELL-SUPPORTED for later digital/vinyl releases; original 1980s commercial LP status still open**

- A 2016 digital release (Creative Media; 27 FLAC files, ~40:08) lists a full cue sequence under Zatsepin’s name and credits the State Cinematography Symphony Orchestra on some metadata trails. Treat as a later commercial issue, not proof of an original 1981 album master.
- A 2022 reconstruction project produced a vinyl + digital album under Zatsepin’s supervision (Shining Sioux / related imprints), recorded at Mosfilm’s Second Music Studio (June–December 2021) with synthesizer work at «Legenda». Track lists include numbers not heard in the finished film (e.g. «Полёт старухи»). Discogs and Russian press document the release and personnel.
- A further limited vinyl+CD repress appears in Discogs dated 2026 (Shining Sioux MA 033-059LP / CD MACD-28).

Sources:
- [Discogs, 2016 digital: Тайна третьей планеты (27×FLAC)](https://www.discogs.com/release/32654880)
- [Discogs master / 2022 reconstruction releases](https://www.discogs.com/master/2641817)
- [Discogs, 2026 limited repress](https://www.discogs.com/release/37569312)
- [Smotrim / Zvuk coverage of 2022 vinyl and digital release](https://smotrim.ru/journal/2747215)

**Version warning (retained):** Do not assume every online upload or English-language release contains the original 1981 music. The Wende Museum explicitly notes a 1995 US dub/re-edit without the original soundtrack. The 2022 album is a reconstruction from score + film stem, not a recovered master.
