# SF-ARM-002 — «Պատվի համար» / *For the Sake of Honor* (1952): Edgar Hovhannisyan cue family

**Status:** PROVISIONAL cue-family lead; multiple extant Armenian Radio Archive recording entries are identified, but film-print relationship and recording-session provenance remain open  
**Region:** Armenian SSR / Yerevan  
**Film:** *For the Sake of Honor* (English archive rendering); Armenian original title and film production record still to be verified  
**Composer credited by the archive:** Edgar Hovhannisyan

## Identified archive objects

The Armenian Radio Archive's Soviet Armenian soundtrack index lists several 1952 entries from *For the Sake of Honor*, all attributed to Edgar Hovhannisyan. Two item pages supply full track metadata:

1. **“Death of Margarit”** — 2:07; composer Edgar Hovhannisyan; conductor Yevgeny Svetlanov; year 1952. https://armradioarchive.am/en/song/margariti-mahy-patvi-hamar-k-f
2. **“Suspicion”** — 1:21; composer Edgar Hovhannisyan; conductor Yevgeny Svetlanov; year 1952. https://armradioarchive.am/en/song/kaskatsank-patvi-hamar-k-f

The same 1950s index also lists **“Elizbarov's Crime”** and **“Song Without Words”** from the same film, both attributed to Hovhannisyan, but item-level metadata for those entries has not yet been captured:
- https://armradioarchive.am/en/genres/soundtrack/1950s
- https://armradioarchive.am/en/genres/soundtrack/?page=5

## Evidence and object distinctions

- **VERIFIED at archive-catalogue level:** at least two named recording entries are separately catalogued with durations, year, composer and conductor.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED:** whether these are recordings made for the original film soundtrack, radio-studio re-recordings, broadcast performances, later preservation transfers or another release context.
- The 1952 year is the archive's entry year; do not assume it is the exact recording date without the archive's date-field definition.
- Do not treat the cue titles as proof of a soundtrack LP or a complete score.

## Next actions

1. Retrieve the original Armenian film title, studio, director, production year and music credit from an Armenfilm catalogue or surviving film credits.
2. Capture item-level pages for “Elizbarov's Crime” and “Song Without Words”; compare their metadata and whether they are distinct performances.
3. Ask the Armenian Radio Archive what its displayed year field means and request carrier, session/broadcast date, original catalogue ID and transfer history.
4. Compare the archive recordings with the film print only under lawful access and only after identifying the exact film version.
5. Preserve Armenian titles/scripts alongside the English archive translations.

## Decision

Add as SF-ARM-002, provisional. It is a new regional film-music cue family with at least two separately catalogued extant audio entries; no listening, contact, copying or rights clearance is claimed.
