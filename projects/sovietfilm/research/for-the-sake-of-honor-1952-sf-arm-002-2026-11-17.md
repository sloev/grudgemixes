# SF-ARM-002 — «Պատվի համար» / *For the Sake of Honor* (film 1956; archive cue pages dated 1952–53)

**Status:** PROVISIONAL cue-family lead; seven exact Armenian Radio Archive item pages are now identified, but their relationship to the 1956 film remains disputed because the archive's displayed years predate the film.  
**Region:** Armenian SSR / Yerevan  
**Film:** «Պատվի համար» / *Patvi hamar* / *For the Sake of Honor*, **1956**  
**Director:** Artashes Hay-Artyan (Արտաշես Հայ-Արտյան)  
**Composer:** Edgar Hovhannisyan, credited by the Armenian Radio Archive on the cue pages  
**Conductor:** Yevgeny Svetlanov, credited by the archive on all seven item pages

## Film chronology correction

The earlier working file title and baseline row treated 1952 as the film year. That is not supported by the Armenian film sources checked here. The GAT Museum's collection catalogue identifies Artashes Hay-Artyan's director's script for «Պատվի համար» as created/produced in Yerevan in **1956**. Contemporary Armenian retrospectives likewise date the film to 1956 and report that Hay-Artyan asked Aram Khachaturian for a score; Khachaturian recommended his young student Edgar Hovhannisyan.

The Armenian Radio Archive's seven cue pages, however, display **1952** for six items and **1953** for one. Those fields are earlier than the documented 1956 film. The pages call the items “from [the] film,” but the archive's date-field definition and source-carrier/session metadata have not been obtained. Possible explanations include an earlier recording/date field, music first prepared for a stage/radio version and later reused, or an archive title/date association problem. None can be selected from current evidence. Do not call these verified 1956 film-print cues until the archive explains the date field and the exact recording/film relationship is checked.

## Separate radio/stage-performance object — not a soundtrack cue

The Armenian Radio Archive's Performance category separately lists Hrachya Nersisyan's “A Part From ‘For The Sake Of Honor’ Drama” dated 1955, duration 2:49. This is a stage/drama performance entry, not one of the seven musical cues above. Keep it separate unless an item-level record proves a direct musical relationship.

## Evidence and object distinctions

- **VERIFIED at archive-catalogue level:** seven separately named recording pages, durations, archive year fields, composer and conductor credits.
- **WELL-SUPPORTED film facts:** the film «Պատվի համար» is dated 1956 in Armenian sources; the GAT Museum holds a 1956 director's script; Armenian retrospective reporting attributes the film's music to Hovhannisyan after Khachaturian's recommendation.
- **DISPUTED / UNRESOLVED:** the seven archive objects are labelled as being from the film, but their 1952/1953 year fields predate the 1956 film. The exact recording/session and film-print relationship is not established.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED:** whether the seven are original film-session recordings, radio-studio re-recordings, earlier stage/radio performances, later preservation transfers or another recording context.
- Do not treat the cue titles as proof of a soundtrack LP or complete score.

## Access and rights

The Armenian Radio Archive provides public web playback for personal, non-commercial listening. Its Terms of Use (last updated July 2026) prohibit copying/downloading for redistribution and state that any use beyond personal listening — including educational, research, broadcast or commercial use — requires prior written permission. No direct audio-file URL or permitted private research download route has been identified. A public player is not a download licence.

## Next actions

1. Ask the Armenian Radio Archive to define the “Year” field for these seven entries and supply item IDs, carrier format, recording/session or broadcast dates, recording location, transfer history and any earlier stage/radio source.
2. Ask whether the seven entries were actually used in the 1956 film, and if so which film version/cue positions; request a cue sheet or production/recording documentation.
3. Compare the archive recordings with a legally accessible 1956 film print only after the item identity is clarified. Do not download or reproduce archive audio without written permission.
4. Preserve Armenian titles/scripts alongside the archive's English translations.
5. Keep the 1955 Hrachya Nersisyan drama performance separate from the seven music items.
