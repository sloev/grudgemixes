# SF-LT-001 — Velnio nuotaka (The Devil's Bride), Lithuanian SSR, 1974/1976

**Programme status:** IN PROGRESS  
**Lead type:** new film-level case-study target; one lead across the film and its related audio editions  
**Region:** Lithuanian SSR  
**Film:** Velnio nuotaka / The Devil's Bride; director Arūnas Žebriūnas; music by Viačeslavas Ganelinas; musical film, 78 minutes  
**Date status:** disputed in cataloguing: Lithuanian Film Centre dates the film 1974; a National Library of Lithuania 2022 programme uses 1976. Do not silently normalize this discrepancy.

## Film and composition identity

The Lithuanian Film Centre's film record lists Velnio nuotaka as a 1974 Lithuanian Film Studio musical film, 78 minutes, with music by Viačeslavas Ganelinas. It credits sound director Stasys Vilkevičius, the State Philharmonic Orchestra and percussion group under Juozas Domarkas, and named singing voices including Asta Chomentauskienė, Viktoras Malinauskas, Vytautas Kernagis and Birutė Dambrauskaitė.

A 2011 *Kauno Diena* interview with Ganelin says he wrote the music in 1972 at the director's request. The interview describes earlier singer attempts: Giedrė Kaukaitė initially sang Jurga, and Ganelin's wife Julija recorded an aria in the studio, but the composer rejected those attempts; Asta Chomentauskienė became the final singer. The film-centre and album credits name Asta, so the earlier attempts are provenance history, not evidence of a released alternate soundtrack. The same interview says the first stage performance of the rhythm-opera took place in 2003 and that a further stage production followed in 2011. Those stage performances are not the 1974 film print.

The 1976 album and the 2000 editions must not be merged with the 1974/1976 film print. The 2000 CD's “sound corrections” do not establish a re-recording. The 2025 audio-description version is a later dub/accessibility layer, not a musical re-score. The planned 2025 live accompaniment is a separate performance version.

## Date and cue-level questions

- **1974 vs 1976:** LFC filmography uses 1974; an LNB 2022 programme uses 1976. The 1976 date also appears on the studio album, but that does not prove that the film itself dates from 1976. The discrepancy remains open pending production, censorship, premiere or print records.
- The film-centre page supplies singer and orchestral credits, but neither it nor the library discography supplies a cue-by-cue score or timestamped track list.
- The 1976 LP is catalogued as a two-record set. The 2000 CD record gives a total duration, not cue names. No score, conductor's parts, recording log or film cue sheet was found in this pass.
- The 2011 interview reports rejected singer attempts, but no surviving test recording is identified; do not create separate audio leads for those attempts.

## Access, provenance and rights

The LNB records document screenings and editions but do not provide an unrestricted online audio stream. The 1976 LP and 2000 CD/cassette are catalogue records, not inspected copies. The LFC film record and 2025 event pages are metadata/announcements; no film print or audio-described track was opened. No audio was played or downloaded, no archive was contacted, and no rights clearance or purchase was made.

## Qualification and next action

This is one qualified regional Soviet film-music lead because the official filmography identifies the film and composer and a public library discography documents distinct 1976 and 2000 audio editions. Count the film once; do not count the LP, CD, cassette, stage productions, audio-description version or live accompaniment as separate new leads.

**Next:** obtain the original film print's sound specification or access route from LFC; locate the 1976 LP label/inner-sleeve images and compare its audio to the film print; obtain the 2000 CD booklet and cassette metadata; resolve 1974/1976 using production and premiere records; seek score/cue sheets and map exact cue boundaries; inspect the 2025 audio-description version's track structure; verify whether the announced 2025 live project occurred and whether a recording exists.
