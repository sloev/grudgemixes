# Armenian Radio Archive — Soviet Armenian film-music recordings (research lead, 2026-11-17)

**Status:** VERIFIED collection-level discovery route; individual cue identity and recording provenance require item-level follow-up  
**Scope:** Soviet Armenian cinema, Armenfilm / Hayfilm, 1952–1986  
**Priority:** High — expands the project beyond its Moscow-centred science-fiction anchors

## Collection discovery

The Armenian Radio Archive's English-language soundtrack index describes **186 recordings**, dated 1952–1986, associated with **27 performers and 18 composers** and music from Soviet Armenian feature films. It identifies Armenfilm (Hayfilm), founded in Yerevan in 1923, as the principal Armenian film studio, and names composers including Konstantin Orbelyan, Arno Babajanian, Artemi Ayvazyan, Tigran Mansurian and Edvard Mirzoyan.

Collection page: https://armradioarchive.am/en/genres/soundtrack  
The index is searchable and includes track-level pages, duration, composer and an audio player. Some track pages have no lyrics or detailed recording-session metadata.

## Item-level test case: Tigran Mansurian, “Melody” from *Ashnan Arev* / *Autumn Sun*

The archive has a dedicated page for “Melody (from ‘Ashnan Arev’ / ‘Autumn Sun’ Film),” attributed to Tigran Mansurian, duration 2:25. The page gives the year as **1986**:
https://armradioarchive.am/en/song/meghedi-ashnan-arev-k-f

However, independent film sources identify *Ashnan Arev* / *Աշնան արև* as a 1977 film directed by Bagrat Oganesyan, with music by Tigran Mansurian:
- Klassiki film page: https://films.klassiki.online/autumn-sun
- Armenian Institute programme note: https://www.armenianinstitute.org.uk/news-text
- Armenian-language report: https://armlur.am/1340272/

This creates an important unresolved date distinction. The archive's “1986” may refer to the recording, a later broadcast/library capture or a metadata error; it cannot be used as the film's production year. A separate 2024 re-recording by Sergey Smbatyan and the Armenian State Symphony Orchestra appears on *Tigran Mansurian: Film & Theatre Music*; that is a later orchestral re-recording and must not be conflated with the Armenian Radio Archive object or original film soundtrack:
https://music.apple.com/us/album/tigran-mansurian-film-theatre-music/1785241663

## Why this matters

The archive supplies a track-level listening route into regional Soviet cinema and a substantial corpus for comparative work on film composition, radio preservation and soundtrack circulation. It also offers a practical path to cue candidates beyond the two current anchor films. The 1977/1986 mismatch is itself a useful provenance problem to resolve rather than silently normalise.
