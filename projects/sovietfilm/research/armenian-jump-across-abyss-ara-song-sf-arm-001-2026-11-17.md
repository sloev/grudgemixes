# SF-ARM-001 — «Прыжок через пропасть» / “Ara's Song” (Armenfilm, 1959; Armenian Radio Archive recording entry, 1960)

**Status:** PROVISIONAL cue-family lead; an extant archive recording is catalogued, but film-year, recording-date and version relationships require reconciliation  
**Region:** Armenian SSR / Yerevan  
**Composer:** Konstantin Orbelyan (Կոնստանտին Օրբելյան / Константин Орбелян)  
**Film title:** «Прыжок через пропасть» (rendered by the Armenian Radio Archive as “Flight Over The Abyss”)  
**Cue/song:** “Ara's Song” / «Արայի երգը» (archive transliteration in URL: *arayi-ergy*)

## Archive objects

The Armenian Radio Archive has multiple item pages for “Ara's Song (from ‘Flight Over The Abyss’ Film)” and attributes the music to Konstantin Orbelyan. The entries should be kept separate rather than merged:
1. **Sergey Davidyan**, 3:26; year 1960; composer Konstantin Orbelyan; conductor Aram Katanyan; no lyricist listed on this page: https://armradioarchive.am/en/song/arayi-ergy-trichk-andundi-vrayov-k-f-89
2. **Sergey Davidyan**, 3:32; year 1960; composer Konstantin Orbelyan; lyrics Hovhannes Ghukasyan; conductor Aram Katanyan: https://armradioarchive.am/en/song/arayi-ergy-trichk-andundi-vrayov-k-f-87
3. **Konstantin Orbelyan**, 3:18; year 1960; composer Konstantin Orbelyan; conductor Algis Zhuraitis: https://armradioarchive.am/en/song/arayi-ergy-trichk-andundi-vrayov-k-f-1914

These are separate catalogue entries with different durations and performer/conductor metadata. It is not yet known whether they are alternate performances, alternate edits, duplicate transfers with timing differences, or different arrangements. No listening comparison has been made.

## Film and composer cross-check

The Armenian Composers Union's official Russian-language biography for Orbelyan lists **1959 — «Прыжок через пропасть»** under his film music. This is a stronger composer-credit source than general film databases:
https://acu.am/?page_id=9486&lang=ru

The Armenian Radio Archive's soundtrack overview and 1960s collection group these tracks under Armenian film music and display the archive year as 1960:
- https://armradioarchive.am/en/genres/soundtrack
- https://armradioarchive.am/en/genres/soundtrack/1960s

A Russian-language secondary film listing gives production year 1959 and a premiere date of 30 July 1962, while the Composers Union biography gives 1959 and the radio item pages give 1960. Preserve these as distinct dates: production/filmography year, premiere/release date and archive recording-entry year. The film's precise production and first-release chronology remains to be checked against Armenfilm records and contemporary press.

## Evidence and interpretation

- **VERIFIED:** the Armenian Radio Archive catalogues at least three extant sound objects under the same cue title, with different performer/conductor/duration fields.
- **VERIFIED:** the Armenian Composers Union credits Orbelyan with music for «Прыжок через пропасть» in 1959.
- **DISPUTED / UNRESOLVED:** whether the 1960 radio archive date is original recording, broadcast, preservation or cataloguing date; whether all three objects derive from the same film performance.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED:** whether the recordings are identical to the original film-print mix, a separate radio session, a commercial recording or a later dub. Do not label them as original film soundtrack masters without evidence.

## Decision

Retain SF-ARM-001 as a provisional regional Soviet-film cue family, with three distinct archive objects. It is a strong regional discovery because the radio archive supplies extant recordings and the Composers Union independently confirms Orbelyan's film-music association. The year and version relationships remain open; no listening, copying, contact or rights clearance is claimed.
