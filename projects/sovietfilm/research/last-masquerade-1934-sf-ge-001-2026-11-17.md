# SF-GE-001 — «უკანასკნელი მასკარადი» / *The Last Masquerade* (1934): Grigol Kiladze film-music archive lead

**Status:** PROVISIONAL archive-object lead; the National Archives of Georgia reports preserved film-music recordings, but item-level identifiers and version provenance are not yet public in the source checked  
**Region:** Georgian SSR / Tbilisi  
**Film:** «უკანასკნელი მასკარადი» (*The Last Masquerade*), 1934  
**Composer credited by the archive:** Grigol Kiladze (გრიგოლ კილაძე)

## Archive evidence

The National Archives of Georgia reports that its phonorepository preserves musical works from films made from the 1930s onward. It identifies the music for «უკანასკნელი მასკარადი» (1934) as among the earliest recordings in the collection and credits Grigol Kiladze. The archive says these audio documents have been held in its phonorepository and are being digitised from older magnetic tape to contemporary digital preservation and access copies.

**Primary archive announcement (Georgian):** https://www.archive.gov.ge/ge/news/erovnul-arqivshi-daculi-kinomusika-cifrul-matarebelzec-sheinaxeba  
**National Archives of Georgia digital film catalogue:** https://kinokatalogi.archive.gov.ge/en

## Object and version caution

The announcement establishes an archival audio-preservation project and names the film and composer, but does not provide an item-level shelfmark, tracklist, recording date, carrier generation, session details or whether the sound is an original film mix, a later music-only transfer or a radio/recording-studio performance. Do not call the object the original 1934 soundtrack master until the archive confirms that relationship.

The film date (1934) and the date of the surviving audio object are separate fields. The film's production and release chronology should be confirmed from the National Archives' film catalogue.

## Next actions

1. Search the Georgian-language National Archives film catalogue for the exact title «უკანასკნელი მასკარადი» and capture the production record.
2. Request the phonorepository's item-level identifier, title/tracklist, carrier, recording and transfer dates, composer-credit source and access conditions.
3. Establish whether the preserved music derives from the original film sound negative, a music-only master, broadcast recording or later copy.
4. Compare any authorised archive audio with a verified film print and preserve the Georgian original title.

## Decision

Add as SF-GE-001, provisional archive-object lead. The National Archives explicitly identifies this film's music among preserved recordings; the exact audio object's provenance remains unresolved. No listening, contact, copying or rights clearance is claimed.
