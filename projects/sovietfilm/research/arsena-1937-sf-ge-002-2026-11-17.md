# SF-GE-002 — «არსენა» / *Arsena* (1937): Grigol Kiladze film-music archive lead

**Status:** PROVISIONAL archive-object lead; the National Archives of Georgia reports preserved film-music recordings, but item-level identifiers and version provenance are not yet public in the source checked  
**Region:** Georgian SSR / Georgia  
**Film:** «არსენა» (*Arsena*), 1937  
**Composer credited by the archive:** Grigol Kiladze (გრიგოლ კილაძე)

## Archive evidence

The National Archives of Georgia identifies the music for «არსენა» (1937) as one of the earliest recordings in its phonorepository's collection of film music. The same archive announcement says the repository holds music from approximately 200 feature films and that older magnetic-tape audio documents are undergoing digitisation.

**Primary archive announcement (Georgian):** https://www.archive.gov.ge/ge/news/erovnul-arqivshi-daculi-kinomusika-cifrul-matarebelzec-sheinaxeba  
**National Archives of Georgia digital film catalogue:** https://kinokatalogi.archive.gov.ge/en

## Object and version caution

The archive's announcement confirms a preserved film-music recording associated with the film and composer, but does not expose an item-level identifier, cue list, recording date, carrier generation or the relationship between the phonorepository object and the original film soundtrack. The 1937 film date must not be copied into the recording-date field without evidence.

Keep this as a separate film object from SF-GE-001 even though both are credited to Kiladze and cited in the same archive announcement. Do not merge them into a single composer collection.
