# `Кин-дза-дза!` / Kin-dza-dza! (1986)

## Identification

- Original title: `Кин-дза-дза!`
- Transliteration: `Kin-dza-dza!` (also rendered `Kin-Dza-Dza`)
- Year: 1986 (Soviet release date is listed as 1 December 1986 by some film databases; verify against primary production records)
- Director: Georgiy Daneliya (Георгий Данелия)
- Production: Mosfilm (Мосфильм)
- Music credit: Giya Kancheli (Гия Канчели)

## Music credit

**Evidence status: WELL-SUPPORTED**

A UCL library film catalogue entry for the film lists “Music: Giia Kancheli”. EOFFTV independently lists “Music: Giya Kancheli”. A Sikorski publisher biography/work catalogue also includes `KIN-DZA-DZA` among Kancheli's film music, identifying it as a 1986 Mosfilm feature.

Sources:
- [UCL Library, DVD catalogue PDF, entry for Kin-dza-dza!](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/library/sites/library/files/bain-graffy-dvds7101-7160.pdf) — film production and music credit.
- [EOFFTV, Kin-dza-dza! (1986)](https://eofftv.com/kin-dza-dza-1986/) — film credits and synopsis.
- [Sikorski, Giya Kancheli biography and work catalogue (PDF)](https://glarean-magazin.ch/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Sikorski-Giya-Kancheli-Biographie-und-Werkverzeichnis.pdf) — lists the film among Kancheli's film music.

## Soundtrack release evidence

**Evidence status: WELL-SUPPORTED for a commercial compilation release; relationship to film-print audio still open**

A 1995 UK Olympia CD compilation, *Giya Kancheli – Film Music* (Olympia OCD 608), includes a dedicated `Kin-Dza-Dza` section (tracks 21–24, total 12:38) performed by the U.S.S.R. Cinematography Symphony Orchestra under Sergei Skripka, with engineering credited to V. Babushkin. The same programme appears on a 2001 Japanese reissue (Volcano/Olympia CPC8-1147). Discogs release pages list the four numbered movements/melodies and credit Mosfilm / Daneliya for the film context.

Sources:
- [Discogs, Giya Kancheli – Film Music, Olympia OCD 608 (1995)](https://www.discogs.com/release/4743167-Giya-Kancheli-Film-Music) — label, catalogue, track list, conductor, orchestra, engineer.
- [Discogs, Giya Kancheli – Film Music, Volcano/Olympia CPC8-1147 (2001 Japan)](https://www.discogs.com/release/23553299-Giya-Kancheli-Film-Music) — reissue track list and credits.
- [AllMusic, Kin-Dza-Dza Giya Kancheli Film Music](https://www.allmusic.com/album/kin-dza-dza-giya-kancheli-film-music-mw0001261371) — secondary listing of a 2001 release date (treat as secondary to the Discogs primary metadata).

**Caution:** This establishes a commercial release of film-associated music under Kancheli’s name with named performers. It does **not** by itself prove that the CD contains the complete original film mix, isolated cues as heard in the theatrical print, or unaltered session masters. Compare durations, cue boundaries and mix against a verified original Russian-language film print before treating the CD as equivalent to the film soundtrack.
