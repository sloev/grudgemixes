## Identification

- **Original title:** «Sõjapäevade laulud»

- **Production:** Eesti Telefilm, Estonian SSR
- **Year:** 1969
- **Form:** music film / concert film
- **Director:** Evald Hermaküla
- **Duration:** 18:53
- **Archive item:** Estonian Film Database (EFIS) record 6306; physical description 2 parts, 35 mm, black-and-white, Russian-language text/dialogue, archive item no. 1852. The record also links National Archives files ERA.R-1590.7.133 and ERA.R-1590.4.644.

The EFIS page labels the object a musical film and notes “film without dialogue” / visualisation of music in its terminology fields. It also identifies ERR as distributor/copyright-holder context and provides an ERR Video Archive viewing option.

## Object boundaries

This record documents a specific 1969 Estonian television music-film object and its declared repertoire. It does **not** prove that the listed songs were newly composed for the film, nor that the film audio uses the exact masters named in the music-sheet fields. The listed works are pre-existing wartime songs or later repertoire; the film's edit, performers, orchestral takes, and any studio/session documentation remain to be established.

Do not split this film into five soundtrack tracks until a viewing copy or production documentation confirms sequence, performers, and exact audio objects.
