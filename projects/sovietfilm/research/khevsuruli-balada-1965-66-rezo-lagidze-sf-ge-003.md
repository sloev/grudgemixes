# SF-GE-003 — «ხევსურული ბალადა» / *Khevsuruli Balada* (1965/1966), Rezo Lagidze

**Status:** IN PROGRESS — qualified Georgian SSR film-music case study.  
**Object level:** one film production and credited score target; not yet an identified cue, soundtrack release or verified film-print audio.

## Film identity and dating

The National Library of Georgia's digital library record for the poster «მხატვრული ფილმი \"ხევსურული ბალადა\"» gives an item issue date of **1965**, credits director Shota Managadze, screenplay writer Giorgi Mdivani and composer **Revaz (Rezo) Lagidze**, but its attached JPEG filename is **SAKHVITI 1966 1-90 30.jpg**. The National Archives of Georgia's filming-location record and Georgian Cinema's local film catalogue both date the production to 1965. A 2021 video listing for the film song gives 1966. Preserve this metadata conflict: 1965 is supported as production/still/catalogue metadata, while 1966 appears in the poster-file name and a later video listing. Do not assume that 1965 is conclusively the first public-release date or split these into two films.

Retain the Georgian title «ხევსურული ბალადა» and the transliteration *Khevsuruli Balada*. “Khevsurian Ballad” is a useful English rendering. The film belongs to Georgian SSR cinema and is a regional case beyond the Moscow-centred catalogue.

## Cue-level breakthrough — candidate SF-GE-003-C1

A regional Georgian cultural article dated 30 November 2025 identifies **«ხმა ამაიღე ფანდურო» / “Xma Amaige Fanduro”** as a song from *Khevsuruli Balada*, attributing it to composer Rezo Lagidze and saying the film's characters performed it. A 2021 Lot.ge video listing names **Sofiko Chiaureli and Zurab Kapanidze** as the performers and labels it as a song from the film. This gives the case a specific cue/song candidate rather than only a film-level composer credit. Both pages returned cache misses when opened; their indexed item text was checked. No video was viewed or listened to. The user-uploaded clip is not treated as an authorized copy or proof that it reproduces the original film-print audio. The 1TV radio programme listing dated 13 July 2026 includes a separate segment titled “music for the film Khevsuruli Balada”; whether it contains this song, a later performance, or discussion is unknown.

**Cue assessment:** WELL-SUPPORTED as a song associated with the film by local secondary metadata; ORIGINAL-PRINT VERSION UNVERIFIED. Keep this as one cue candidate within SF-GE-003, not a separate new lead. The attribution to Lagidze should remain provisional until a score, cue sheet, print credit or contemporary source is located.

## Music-object boundary

The National Library poster record provides a film-level composer credit, not a cue list or proof of a standalone soundtrack. Rezo Lagidze's broader filmography includes this film, and a Georgian First Channel / 1TV radio programme archive page dated 13 July 2026 lists a segment titled «მუსიკა ფილმისთვის „ხევსურული ბალადა“» (“Music for the film *Khevsuruli Balada*”). That radio item is a research route to later discussion or excerpts; it is not the original film soundtrack and was not played in this pass.

No score manuscript, orchestral parts, cue sheet, original soundtrack master, standalone release or authorised film-print audio was located in this pass. One cue/song title, «ხმა ამაიღე ფანდურო», is now identified through regional secondary sources, but its film-print version remains unverified. Do not infer instrumentation, folk-song use or cue content from the title or the film's regional setting.
