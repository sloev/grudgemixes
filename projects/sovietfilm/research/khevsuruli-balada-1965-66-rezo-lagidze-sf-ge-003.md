# SF-GE-003 — «ხევსურული ბალადა» / *Khevsuruli Balada* (1965/1966), Rezo Lagidze

**Status:** IN PROGRESS — qualified Georgian SSR film-music case study.  
**Object level:** one film production and credited score target; not yet an identified cue, soundtrack release or verified film-print audio.

## Film identity and dating

The National Library of Georgia's digital library record for the poster «მხატვრული ფილმი "ხევსურული ბალადა"» gives an issue date of **1965** and identifies director Shota Managadze, screenplay writer Giorgi Mdivani and composer **Revaz (Rezo) Lagidze**. The National Archives of Georgia's film-still record also places the production in 1965. Secondary film catalogues commonly list the released film as **1966** and credit Rezo Lagidze as composer. Preserve the distinction: 1965 is supported as poster/production-year metadata; 1966 appears to be the release year. Do not split these into two films.

Retain the Georgian title «ხევსურული ბალადა» and the transliteration *Khevsuruli Balada*. “Khevsurian Ballad” is a useful English rendering. The film belongs to Georgian SSR cinema and is a regional case beyond the Moscow-centred catalogue.

## Music-object boundary

The National Library poster record provides a film-level composer credit, not a cue list or proof of a standalone soundtrack. Rezo Lagidze's broader filmography includes this film, and a Georgian First Channel / 1TV radio programme archive page dated 13 July 2026 lists a segment titled «მუსიკა ფილმისთვის „ხევსურული ბალადა“» (“Music for the film *Khevsuruli Balada*”). That radio item is a research route to later discussion or excerpts; it is not the original film soundtrack and was not played in this pass.

No cue title, score manuscript, orchestral parts, cue sheet, original soundtrack master, standalone release or authorised film-print audio was located in this pass. Do not infer instrumentation, folk-song use or cue content from the title or the film's regional setting.
