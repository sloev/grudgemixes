# SF-AZ-001 — «Abşeron ritmləri» / *Rhythms of Absheron* (1970)

**Status:** IN PROGRESS — WELL-SUPPORTED film-concert identification; original film-print audio and cue boundaries not verified.  
**Object level:** one Azerbaijani SSR full-length musical television film / film-concert. Do not count the ten listed music items as ten separate soundtrack recordings or as a standalone album.

## Production identity

The Azerbaijani National Library's Polad Bülbüloğlu biographical database states that in **1970** he composed music for the full-length musical television film **«Abşeron ritmləri»**. The Azerbaijan State Film Fund's 16 January 2026 Eldar Quliyev profile independently identifies «Abşeron ritmləri» as the director's one full-length musical film. These two local institutional sources support the film-level composer/director relationship.

Kinobiz.az's film record identifies:
- Original title: **«Abşeron ritmləri»**
- English title: *Rhythms of Absheron*
- First-view date: **3 May 1970**
- Duration: **63 minutes**
- Studio: **Azerbaijanfilm**
- Director: **Eldar Guliyev**
- Screenwriter: **Imran Gasimov**
- Cinematographer: **Rasim Ojagov**
- Production designer: **Kamil Najafzadeh**
- Sound operator: **Vladimir Savin**
- Type: **film-concert**
- Order: **Central Television**
- Locations: Baku, Goygol, Sheki, Ganja and the Caspian Sea.

Kinobiz is a specialist Azerbaijani cinema database rather than the original film credit roll. The date, duration and detailed crew data should be checked against an original print or Filmfond catalogue before being treated as primary production metadata.

## Music and performance inventory (Kinobiz record)

Kinobiz lists the following ten musical items as played in the film. Its page supplies English titles/translations; original Azerbaijani title spellings and timecodes are not supplied in the record.

1. **“World Without You”** — composition: Rauf Hajiyev; lyrics: Onegin Hajigasimov; performed by Rashid Behbudov.
2. **“My Azerbaijan”** — composition: Rauf Hajiyev; performed by Rashid Behbudov.
3. **“Oh Dream” / “Dream”** — composition: Ogtay Kazimi; lyrics: Fikret Goja; performed by Rashid Behbudov.
4. **“Oh Life, How Strange You Are”** — composition: Ogtay Kazimi; lyrics: Jabir Novruz; performed by Gaya Vocal-Instrumental Ensemble.
5. **“Intizar”** — composition: Ogtay Kazimi; lyrics: R. Heydar; performed by Flora Karimova.
6. **“Loyalty”** — composition: Alakbar Tagiyev; lyrics: Suleyman Rustam; performed by Zeynab Khanlarova.
7. **Adagio from the ballet “Seven Beauties”** — composition: Gara Qarayev; performed by Rafiga Akhundova and Vladimir Pletnyov.
8. **“When You Speak” / “I Hear Your Voice”** — composition: Tofig Babayev; lyrics: Nabi Khazri; performed by the Gaya Vocal-Instrumental Ensemble.
9. **“Rocks” / “Old Rocks”** — composition: Khayyam Mirzazadeh; lyrics: Anar Rzayev; performed by Ogtay Aghayev.
10. **“Thought”** — composition: Rafig Babayev; performed by the quartet under Rafig Babayev's direction.

**Credit boundary:** Polad Bülbüloğlu is the film-level composer according to the Azerbaijani National Library biography. The Kinobiz item list attributes the individual songs/works to several other composers. Do not overwrite the film-level credit with the song-level credits, or assume that every listed work was newly composed for this film. Some may be pre-existing repertoire incorporated into the film-concert. The exact opening/closing credits, song order, original Azerbaijani titles and timecodes remain unverified.
