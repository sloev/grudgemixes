# SF-UZ-001 — “Do‘ppi tikdim” / «Дўппи тикдим» from *Мафтунингман* (*Maftuningman*, 1958)

**Research status:** RESEARCHED-OPEN (2026-11-18)  
**Lead type:** one regional Uzbek film-song cue family; not a soundtrack album.  
**Cue identity:** “Do‘ppi tikdim” (full song title in the Composers Union biography: “Do‘ppi tikdim ipaklari tillodan”).  
**Performer:** Klara Jalilova (Клара Жалилова), supported by the Uzbek Composers’ Union biography; a 2019 RizaNova video title also identifies her but the video page failed to load.  
**Lyricist:** Turob To‘la (Туроб Тўла).  
**Composer attribution:** unresolved/shared. A 2014 printed score credits “Monas Leviev” (Монас Левиев); the Uzbek Composers’ Union biography places the song among Mutal Burhonov’s film songs and says Burhonov collaborated with M. Leviev and I. Akbarov on songs for *Maftuningman*. Do not assign a sole composer without further primary evidence.

## Film and cue identity

The local-language Shaxs.uz filmography identifies *Maftuningman* as a 1958 musical comedy produced at Uzbekfilm, directed by Yo‘ldosh A‘zamov and Mikhail Krasnyanskiy, and lists Klara Jalilova and Manas Leviyev among the cast. It names “Do‘ppi tikdim” among the film’s songs. TelecomTV’s current Milliy streaming catalogue independently lists the film as a 1958 Uzbek comedy/music title. Neither listing proves the audio source of a particular online excerpt; the film itself was not watched in this pass.

A 2014 Uzbek music-teaching manual by Gulnora Sharipova and G‘ayrat Najmekdinov prints “Do‘ppi tikdim” on pp. 168–172, explicitly labelled “(‘Maftuningman’ kinofilmidan)” and “Yulduz va qizlar qo‘shig‘i.” The printed score credits Turob To‘la’s poem and “Monas Leviev” music. This is a later score witness, not an original 1958 production score or a recording.

The Uzbek Composers’ Union biography of Mutal Burhonov identifies “Do‘ppi tikdim ipaklari tillodan” as a song performed by Klara Jalilova, with Turob To‘la’s words, and says Burhonov collaborated with M. Leviev and I. Akbarov on songs for *Maftuningman*. The biography is strong institutional secondary evidence for performer and the collaborative film-music context, but it does not resolve whether the specific song’s music was solely by Leviev, co-written with Burhonov, or differently credited in the original production materials. The printed score’s “Monas Leviev” credit must be preserved alongside the association’s Burhonov attribution.

A RizaNova YouTube search result identifies a video titled “Klara Jalilova - Do‘ppi tikdim (Maftuningman filmidan)” (video ID lC3B5mHE27U). The direct video page failed to load. Treat it only as a possible film-derived digital excerpt, not as verified original film-print audio, a soundtrack release, or proof of rights.
