# SF-KZ-001 — «Гәкку» (Gakku), vocal cue in «Қыз Жібек» (Kyz-Zhibek, 1970)

**Project:** Soviet Film Music  
**Status:** IN PROGRESS — qualified new regional cue (2026-11-18)  
**Object type:** original film-print audio cue; no standalone soundtrack release established.

## Film and cue identity

The official Kazakhfilm history page dates Sultan-Akhmet Khodzhikov's film «Қыз Жібек» to 1970. The cue under investigation is the vocal song **«Гәкку» (Gakku)**, attributed to singer **Мунира Ижанова (Munira Izhánova)**. In a 2014 interview, Izhánova recalls being invited to Kazakhfilm after Nurgisa Tlendiev heard her voice, being asked to sing Gakku, and later being called to participate in the film. A 2026 El.kz article states that she recorded Zhibek's Gakku offscreen in 1969. This supports a reported **1969 recording / 1970 film release** chronology; no exact studio-session date or original recording log has been located.

The 2026 Saryn scholarly article distinguishes the folk song «Гәкку» (associated with Үкілі Ыбырай / Ükili Ybyrai) from Tlendiev's separate instrumental kui **«Аққу» (Akku)**. Kazakhfilm's own stars page calls Tlendiev's «Аққу» the musical symbol of «Қыз Жібек». Do not merge these two cue families: SF-KZ-001 is the vocal Gakku performance attributed to Izhánova, not the instrumental Akku.

## Film-print / release / version boundaries

- **Target:** the original Kazakh-language film-print audio of «Қыз Жібек» (1970), with Gakku sung offscreen by Izhánova.
- **Standalone soundtrack release:** none identified in the catalogue/search pass; this is not evidence that no release ever existed.
- **Later re-recordings or reconstructions:** not established in this pass. The underlying folk song and Tlendiev's separate Akku kui are not substitutes for the film-print cue.
- **Later dubs:** not checked. Do not assume the 1970 Kazakh-language print's vocal attribution carries over to later Russian or other-language dubs.
- **Cue position/timecode:** not yet checked against an authorised film print. The interview and later article provide attribution, not a timecoded cue sheet.

## Evidence and limitations

The singer's 2014 interview is first-person recollection of the selection and studio work. The 2026 El.kz article supplies the explicit 1969 date and offscreen attribution. The official Kazakhfilm history page identifies the 1970 release; its stars page separately identifies Akku as Tlendiev's instrumental film symbol. The 2026 Saryn article gives the Gakku/Akku distinction and cites a contemporaneous 23 January 1969 *Vechernyaya Alma-Ata* item (“Сегодня на «Казахфильме»”). Searches of regional web and library catalogues did not locate a scan of that newspaper item in this pass.

No official full-film stream or item-level print access was verified. Public user-uploaded copies were not treated as authorised access. No audio was auditioned; no film or audio file was downloaded; no contact, permission or rights clearance was made.

Keep this as one cue-level new lead; do not count Akku as a second lead.

## 2026-11-18 follow-up — 23 January 1969 newspaper access audit

The Saryn article remains the only source located in this pass that cites the contemporaneous item “Сегодня на «Казахфильме»,” *Вечерняя Алма-Ата*, 23 January 1969. A local newspaper-history article published 29 December 2021 confirms that the city paper was relaunched in early 1968 under the title «Вечерняя Алма-Ата», so the 1969 masthead/title is chronologically plausible. This corroborates the periodical identity, not the cited article's contents or the exact issue's survival.

A National Academic Library PDF surfaced in the catalogue search but proved to be an Akim Tarazi biobibliography with a periodicals index, not a newspaper holdings/issue catalogue. Discard it as an access route. Searches of the current newspaper site and indexed regional library/archive pages did not locate a scan or digitised copy of the 23 January 1969 issue. No holding institution for that exact issue has been verified.

**Result:** no new audio cue, no timecode, no cue-sheet or studio-log evidence, and no status change to SF-KZ-001. The next step is a targeted holdings enquiry to the National Library of Kazakhstan / Almaty municipal archive and, separately, the Kazakhfilm production archive for the 23 January 1969 issue and any music cue sheet or Gakku recording log. No enquiry was sent in this pass.
