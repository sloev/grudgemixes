# SF-AUD-010 — «Гәкку» in «Қыз Жібек» (1970)

Status: IDENTITY UNRESOLVED

**Object:** original Kazakh-language film-print audio cue, vocal song «Гәкку» (Gakku), attributed to Munira Izhánova; reported recording date 1969.

**Evidence:** Izhánova's 2014 interview describes her selection and studio work for the film. A 2026 El.kz article explicitly attributes the offscreen Gakku performance to her in 1969. Official Kazakhfilm dates the film to 1970. The official stars page and 2026 Saryn article identify Tlendiev's «Аққу» (Akku) as a separate instrumental cue; do not conflate it with Gakku.
