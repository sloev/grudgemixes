# SF-AUD-009 — Stalker (1979), original film-print theme

Status: STREAM ONLY

**Object:** 1979 film-print mono audio, specifically the final theme in Stalker's dream and opening titles (working cue description; no official cue title/timecode established).

**Evidence:** Artemyev's first-person account says this final theme was retained for the dream and opening titles and used synthesizer, tar and flute. The rejected “Pulcherrima Rosa” prototype is not the released film cue. Mosfilm's official page lists a 2025 4K, 163-minute, mono restoration and a viewing route.

**Boundary:** This is the film-print audio, not the separately catalogued later “Сталкер: Медитация” item SF-AUD-005. The 2025 restoration is not yet proven to be audio-identical to the original 1979 release.

**Access/terms:** Official viewing route listed; playback and cue timecodes not yet checked. No file download, copying or rights clearance. Do not purchase or copy without owner approval.
