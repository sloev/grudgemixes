# Soviet Film Music

A collaborative research archive investigating music written for Soviet cinema, with an initial emphasis on science-fiction films and animation. The long-term ambition is a richly sourced website; for now, this repository is a **Markdown-first research workspace**.

The project is interested in the music itself: composers, scores, songs, cues, performers, orchestras, studios, recording practices, instruments and electronics, sound design, surviving recordings, soundtrack releases, and the cultural/production contexts that shaped them.

## Initial focus

- **Кин-дза-дза! / Kin-dza-dza!** (1986) — directed by Georgiy Daneliya; music by Giya Kancheli.
- **Тайна третьей планеты / The Mystery of the Third Planet** (1981) — directed by Roman Kachanov; music by Aleksandr Zatsepin.

These are starting points, not boundaries. Explore Soviet science-fiction cinema, animated features and shorts, fantasy, fairy tales, adventure, and other films whose music or sound worlds are revealing.

## Editorial rules

- This is research, not a fan wiki: distinguish verified credits from interpretation and leads.
- Never invent a cue list, instrument, recording date, performer, or soundtrack release.
- A film credit for “music” does not by itself tell us what was recorded, what survives, or whether a commercial soundtrack exists.
- Treat film audio, soundtrack albums, re-recordings, dubs, restorations and fan uploads as distinct objects.
- Record source URLs, access dates, exact titles and relevant page/track references.
- Respect copyright and archive terms. Research notes may link to material; do not upload copyrighted audio, scans or artwork without permission.
- The future website is downstream of the evidence. Do not build it yet.
