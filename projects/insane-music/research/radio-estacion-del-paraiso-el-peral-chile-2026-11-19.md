# Radio Estación del Paraíso / La furia de los caballos sin patas — Chile screening lead (2026-11-19)

## Status

**C — screening/context lead only; not a main-discography candidate yet.** A patient-run radio project and an audio-bearing documentary are documented, but no exact participant-created song, broadcast item, or independently issued soundtrack track has yet been identified.

## Distinguish the recording objects

### 1. Radio Estación del Paraíso broadcasts

The radio began at Hospital Psiquiátrico El Peral in Santiago in 1999. A contemporary 2000–2001 civic-programme case study describes the station as produced and operated by patients. At the October 2000 Santiago Amable event, it broadcast both pre-recorded and live material, including songs and poems; the account describes twelve participants and ten inpatients with their own programme sections. This establishes music-bearing broadcasts, but gives no song titles, composers, performers, carrier IDs or track-level credits.

Nicolás Morales Sáez's 2023 doctoral thesis describes the radio's trajectory as El Peral (1999–2002), Hospital Barros Luco (2002–2003), then community radio / Radio Tierra (2003–2006). It says recordings were edited into microprogrammes. The thesis could not trace the microprogrammes at their last reported location, Barros Luco, or in Radio Tierra's records; it notes that Radio Tierra programmes had been transferred to the Archivo Nacional under the “Archivos de Mujeres y Géneros” project, but this programme was not found in the relevant holdings.

### 2. Documentary: *La furia de los caballos sin patas*

CineChile catalogues the documentary as a 2001 Chilean production, 67 minutes, colour, DVCam / 16 mm, directed by Francisco Schultz, with sound by Roberto Martí; its national premiere is listed as 29 November 2001. It documents the first two years of the radio project.

A 1 September 2011 OCEP post, reproducing an account by Rubén Nilo (psychologist and member of the 1999–2000 radio team), describes a soundtrack comprising radio transmissions plus unreleased tracks by Shogun, Tobías Alcayota and “Joe Villablanca”. A public YouTube upload by activaclavecerebrotv, dated 19 August 2024, credits music by Shogun, Tobías Alcayota and “Jeo Villablanca”, and labels the documentary “Chile, 2004”. Preserve the Joe/Jeo spelling and 2001/2004 date differences; the latter may represent a later version or catalogue error, not a second film. The YouTube page is a stream listing, not a standalone soundtrack or download route.

The soundtrack is not yet a verified album: no label, catalogue number, format, complete tracklist, cue sheet or independent carrier record has been found. The named musicians are soundtrack contributors; their tracks must not be mistaken for music composed or performed by radio participants.

### 3. Original cassettes and discs

The 2023 thesis quotes Rubén Nilo describing a 2003 lockout at Barros Luco after which the team could not retrieve its equipment, cassettes or discs. The thesis independently reports that the microprogrammes' last known location was Barros Luco and that the researcher could not locate them in Radio Tierra / Archivo Nacional records. Treat this as a documented access and custody problem, not proof that every original recording was destroyed. No contact or archive enquiry was made in this pass.

## Evidence and editorial assessment

- **Institutional setting:** supported by the contemporary civic-programme case study, CineChile and the 2023 thesis.
- **Music-bearing content:** supported at programme level (songs and poems) and in the documentary soundtrack description; no exact participant-created composition or track has been identified.
- **Authorship/performance:** unresolved. Do not infer that every song in the broadcasts was written or performed by patients.
- **Documentary soundtrack:** named external contributors are reported, but the exact tracklist and relation between the film mix and any standalone soundtrack are unverified.
- **Access:** a public documentary stream is listed at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yKCQ6I0AK6E; no direct audio file or authorised download route identified. The documentary page's 2004 label conflicts with the 2001 CineChile record.
- **Rights:** unknown. A public stream or historical broadcast does not grant reuse rights.
- **Obscurity:** not rated; the documentary and project have some public/academic visibility, while the survival and circulation of the underlying broadcasts remain unknown.

**Do not count this as a new core recording or assign a catalogue ID yet.** It is a promising archive-recovery lead only. The editorial priority is to identify specific music and its creators, not to collect diagnostic narratives or anonymous participant identities.
