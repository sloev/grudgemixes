# IM-011 — ARSCH, *ARSCH* (2020): recording and provenance audit

**Baseline status:** RESEARCHED-OPEN  
**Pathways:** C — self-taught/autodidactic practice; D — DIY/home-studio and independent release; E — distinctive collective musical practice  
**Place:** Bitsch, Valais, Switzerland  
**Object:** 18-track self-titled release, 2020; cassette and digital editions

## Exact release identity

The artist's Bandcamp page identifies an 18-track *ARSCH* album released on 1 December 2020. It offers a limited cassette edition and digital album. The cassette uses professionally dubbed Type-I C-44 tapes, is hand-numbered, and has a different arrangement/mix from the digital edition. Tracks and durations are listed on the artist page:
https://arsch.bandcamp.com/album/arsch

The issuing label Empty Space identifies the release as catalogue **ES-001**, 2020, edition of 100, and describes the cassette and digital edition as compiled from recordings that remained after the commune's barn fire:
https://www.empty-space.ch/work/arsch-arsch

A Japanese specialist retailer's product record independently lists the cassette as an 18-track 2020 Empty Space release, edition of 100, and repeats the label's tracklist and provenance account:
https://tobirarecords.com/products/arsch-arsch-tape

## Community and recording context

The label and artist page describe ARSCH as a self-sufficient anarcho-syndicalist commune running a farm in Bitsch, with a 1970s-style recording studio in an old barn. The collective's loosely assembled (non-)musicians recorded improvisations on reel-to-reel tapes stored in the barn. A 2019 fire destroyed most of the vintage instruments and a substantial portion of the tape archive; the 2020 album compiled surviving recordings.

The label's description is a first-party release account, not an independent oral-history investigation. Do not repeat the fire's cause as a verified fact beyond the label's account. No individual member biographies or detailed session logs are supplied in the sources checked.

## Version and survival distinctions

- The 2020 album is a curated selection of remaining recordings, not the commune's complete tape archive.
- The cassette and digital editions have different track arrangements/mixes; record them as separate release versions.
- The source reel-to-reel archive was partly destroyed, but the released album and the limited cassette edition are extant recording objects.
- The album's 18 tracks range from short fragments to longer improvisations. The release descriptions do not provide original recording dates or reel-by-reel provenance.

## Insane Music fit

This baseline item qualifies through documented communal DIY practice, self-organised production, a barn recording studio, a surviving tape archive and a distinctive collective musical practice. It is not an institutional psychiatric record and no psychiatric history is asserted. The project's editorial description should avoid turning the band's own “non-musicians” wording into a value judgement.

## Access and rights

The artist's Bandcamp page offers authorised streaming and a digital edition; the label page identifies the release and edition. These pages do not establish reuse rights for the underlying reel recordings or permission to republish audio. No listening comparison of cassette and digital versions, contact, purchase or rights clearance is claimed in this audit.

## Next actions

1. Compare the cassette and digital track orders/mixes through permitted listening and record the exact differences.
2. Request track-level source-reel dates, recording personnel and editing/compilation notes from Empty Space or the collective.
3. Determine how many source reels survived the 2019 fire and whether any unissued material is accessible with permission.
4. Keep the 2020 release separate from the earlier commune recording archive and from any later reissues.

## Decision

The baseline lead is RESEARCHED-OPEN: the exact 2020 release, cassette edition, tracklist, community context and partial archive loss are supported by the artist and label sources. Recording dates, source-reel mapping, cassette/digital mix differences and rights remain open.
