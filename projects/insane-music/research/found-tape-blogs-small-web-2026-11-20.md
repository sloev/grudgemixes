# Found-tape blogs and small-web archives: discovery map (2026-11-20)

**Purpose:** sources for Vol. 10 *Found* and for the *Unwanted* and *Made Anyway* strands. These are discovery infrastructure, not proof of provenance (method §3C). Never treat a blog rip as permission. Apply the found-tape rule: trace the maker, post an "is this yours?" notice, hold royalties, and never use private speech.

Already in the repo before this pass: demo-tapes-brasil.blogspot.com, cambodiancassettearchives.blogspot.com, heavyleatherblog.wordpress.com (cassette archive), The Cassette Archive (Ezra Fike), Intertapes.

## Strangers' home tapes (closest to "found")

| Site | What it is | Era | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EclectikTronik Found Tapes** — https://eclectiktronik.wordpress.com/found-tapes-archive-cassettes/ | Archive begun 1985 of strangers' home recordings, made by microphone onto tapes found in skips and bins, or left in machines bought at car-boot, attic and jumble sales (UK). Each entry gives an estimated date and the find place, e.g. a 1971 tape from a Liscard (Wirral) attic sale. | UK, 1960s–80s tapes | **Top priority.** Find location and date per tape. Screen for music, not speech. |
| **Tape Findings** (RJ Porter / "Sweet Thunder") | Weekly excerpts from one-of-a-kind cassettes bought at garage sales (MetaFilter, 2007); home recordings, demos and oddities by unknown people. Original site likely dead; use the Wayback Machine and the Lostwave's Finest wiki page. | US, 2000s blog | Check the Wayback snapshots of weeks 15, 49, 73 and 86, which MetaFilter singled out. |
| **The Cassette Archive** (Ezra Fike, Bandcamp) | Thrift-store tapes from West Plains, Missouri and elsewhere, choosing tapes with no online footprint. | US | Already noted; interviewed at Anomaly Index. |
| **Intertapes** | Crowdsourced archive of found cassettes from thrift stores, attics and flea markets, with scans and full audio. | Worldwide | Already noted; filter by country to find non-US tapes. |
| **Anomaly Index — "Found Sound" category** — https://anomalyindex.com/category/found-sound/ | A blog about thrift-store tapes: interviews with diggers (Fike; Bennett Williamson, who played three hours of thrift-store tapes on WFMU in 2008) and posts on single tapes. | US, 2020– | Good for collector contacts. |
| **Tapes I Found At The Thrift Store** (YouTube) | Archive of discarded home videos. | US | Video; mostly private speech, so context only. |

## Radio shows and old blog networks that aired found tapes

| Site | What it is | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **WFMU's Beware of the Blog** (archive) | No longer updated, but the archive is still full of articles and MP3s; searchable. | Search "found", "cassette", "thrift", "home recording". |
| **WFMU playlists:** *Lost Media Archive* (BC Sterrett), *Friendly Persuasion* (Otis Fodder), *Radio Boredcast* (People Like Us) | Sterrett's show covers found sounds, private press and outsider music. One *Radio Boredcast* playlist features Daniel Menche's found home acetates of the 1940s–50s, made by ordinary families. | Playlists name the tapes and find places. |
| **WFMU *Bodega Pop*** (Gary Sullivan) | Music ripped from cassettes and CDs found in immigrant-run corner shops. | **High value for diversity.** Non-US diaspora tapes. |
| **inches-per-second.blogspot.com** | Reel-to-reel found-tape blogger, formerly at WFMU's blog. | Reels, 1950s–70s. |
| **Lo Finest radio shows** — https://archive.org/details/lofinest | Charles Rice Goff III's program on homemade 1980s–90s cassettes. | Hometaper scene, international. |
| **NOISE-ARCH** — https://archive.org/details/noise-arch | Tapes donated in 2009 by former CKLN host Myke Dyer (Toronto). | Bulk download; Canadian and international underground. |
