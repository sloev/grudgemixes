# IM-054 — 은평병원 아트브뤼트 뮤지크: patient-created song audio corpus (2012–2019)

**Programme status:** IN PROGRESS  
**Catalogue status:** PARTIALLY VERIFIED  
**Pathway:** A — Institutional music  
**Environment:** Eunpyeong Hospital (은평병원), Seoul, South Korea  
**Recording form:** Hospital-blog audio corpus; individual carriers and item URLs not yet identified

## Evidence for extant recordings

The Seoul Metropolitan Government's 2014 hospital notice describes the Art Brut Musique festival and says the hospital blog would publish the creative process, song audio and photographs. A 2015 *Maeil Business Newspaper* report on the sixth festival states that 13 songs were presented and names two works: **“우리들의 약”** and **“소중한 삶으로.”** These are reported festival titles; this pass did not verify that either exact item is among the blog audio.

A 21 February 2019 *Newsis* report says the festival began in 2012, was then at its tenth edition, and explicitly states that the hospital's **아트브뤼트뮤지크 블로그** carried audio recordings of songs created by participants and photographs. This is direct institutional reporting that a corpus of recordings existed online. The linked blog is http://blog.naver.com/artbrutmusic; Naver blocked direct inspection in this pass, so no individual post, audio file, item date, performer credit or recording medium was verified.

## Admission and deduplication

This is counted as **one corpus-level lead**, not thirteen song leads and not one lead per annual festival. The evidence supports patient-created songs and blog-hosted audio, but it does not yet support an item-by-item discography. Do not split “우리들의 약,” “소중한 삶으로,” or any other festival song into separate core entries until a stable item-level audio post is found.

The work is described as created by people participating in hospital music programmes. The notes do not reproduce diagnostic labels from press coverage, infer individual diagnoses, or identify individual participants. This is institutional music, not a claim that every participant is an outsider musician.

## Access, consent and rights

- The official Seoul page links the Naver blog; direct blog access was blocked by the platform in this pass.
- The 2019 report attests that audio was posted, but no individual recording was opened or played.
- No participant consent, performer-credit policy, copyright ownership, reuse permission or archive preservation status was established.
- No contact was made and no permission was requested. The Seoul page's public-sector text reuse notice does not grant rights in the songs or recordings.

## Next exact action

With owner approval, seek stable item-level blog URLs or an institutional export; record each song's original title, date, group/performer credit, recording medium, provenance and any consent/rights conditions. Confirm whether the 2015 titles are present in the blog corpus. Keep the corpus as one lead until individual recordings are independently identified.
