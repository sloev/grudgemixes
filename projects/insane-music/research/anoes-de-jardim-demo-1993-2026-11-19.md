# IM-050 — Anões de Jardim, Demo (1993)

**Status:** PARTIALLY VERIFIED / PROMISING  
**Pathways:** F — locally marginal/underground scene; D — DIY cassette production and circulation.  
**Count:** One recording object (the eight-track demo), not eight separate leads.

## Identity and recording

The band’s preserved Real Deal page says Anões de Jardim formed in January 1993, with Frederico P. Gomes, Felipe P. Gomes, Alexandre Velozo and Eurico Ludwig. It records the band’s first official performance at Curitiba’s 92 Degrees venue and says the first demo-tape was recorded live in Studio Solo in July 1993, with eight songs and weak distribution in the independent market.

A specialist collector’s post dated 11 December 2012 identifies a 1993 Curitiba hardcore demo, recorded live at Studio Solo, and attributes the surviving copy to the collector’s private collection. It lists eight tracks and 18:51 total duration. The collector describes transfer instability, a 50-second lead-in on side B and a cut final track, “Bells Bit”. This is item-level evidence that a cassette copy and digitised programme were reported as extant; the audio itself was not inspected or auditioned in this research pass.

## Scene and admission rationale

A contemporaneous Folha de Londrina report dated 4 July 1997 lists Anões de Jardim among six Curitiba bands appearing at the fifth National Garage festival. The same article describes the 92 Degrees venue, the independent Bloody label, Studio Solo and the city’s alternative festival circuit. Together with the band’s own statement that the first demo had weak independent distribution, this supports pathway F/D as a specific local DIY/underground recording history.

The band later had better-distributed releases and an MTV clip, but those facts relate to the 1994 demo and later activity, not to the 1993 first demo. Keep those recording objects separate. This is not a psychiatric or diagnostic case and should not be described as such.

## Deduplication and status

- One lead: the July 1993 eight-track demo.
- Do not count the eight tracks, multiple tape sides, the collector’s copy and the blog entry separately.
- Distinct from G.D.E. (Santa Maria, 1995/1996), and distinct from Anões de Jardim’s 1994 Extra! and 1995 third demo.
- Admission: PROMISING, not VERIFIED. The band’s own history and 1997 local press corroborate the scene; the collector post supplies a concrete item record and tracklist. The physical carrier, exact label/cover, full transfer chain and audio content remain unverified.
