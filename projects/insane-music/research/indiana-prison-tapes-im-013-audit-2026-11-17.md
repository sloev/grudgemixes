# IM-013 — Indiana State Prison tape provenance reconciliation (2026-11-17)

**Baseline lead status:** RESEARCHED-OPEN  
**Subject:** Bruce Jackson's original Indiana State Prison field tapes, Michigan City, 1962

## Baseline identity retained

The existing Insane Music environment-lead record identifies Bruce Jackson's recordings at Indiana State Prison on 29–31 March, 20–25 April and 1–3 July 1962. It gives Indiana University Archives of Folk and Primitive Music accessions **62-4-28 (11), 62-6-18 (21), 62-8-9 (27)** and original tape numbers **T-7-inch 465–498, 503, 539–543**. The *Death Chants* Folk-Legacy editor's note says the tapes contain songs omitted from the LP. It also distinguishes Hank Ferguson performances re-recorded at his home in July 1963 from the original prison sessions.

The exact primary sources still needed are Indiana University's accession register/tape logs and the original edition notes. The existing record should not imply that all tape contents are unreleased or that every Smithsonian copy derives from the Indiana University accessions.

## Newly discovered separate Smithsonian objects

This research pass found two other accession groups in the Smithsonian Ralph Rinzler Folklife Archives:

1. **IM-044 — FW-ASCH-7RR-4919 / FW-ASCH-7RR-4918.** Two separate items titled “Bruce Jackson Field Recordings: Indiana Prison Songs” (reels 1 and 2). Smithsonian search records identify named performers and tracklists; reel 1 has a 1963 Indiana event note. The archive also describes CDR copies. These are not the same as the 1962 IU accessions merely because they share recorder, region and subject.
2. **IM-045 — FW-ASCH-7RR-1966.** A separate item titled “Bruce Jackson Field Recordings: Prison Songs,” one 7-inch analogue reel, place Indiana/Bloomington, with no date, facility, tracklist or performers in the search record. The digits “1966” belong to the local number and are not a recording date.

## Reconciliation outcome

The 1962 IU accession series remains a distinct, dated field-recording group. The Smithsonian 1963 pair and 7RR-1966 reel are separate archival objects until item-level provenance proves a duplication or transfer relationship. Do not merge these objects or assign 1962/1963/1966 dates by analogy.

The Smithsonian Asch records include restrictions against duplication and research-only listening/viewing on related items, and publication permission is required. Check the restriction attached to each exact item before arranging access. The Indiana University access route and terms have not yet been independently checked.

## Additional item-level cross-check (2026-11-17)

A Library of Congress finding aid for the Bruce Jackson and Diane Christian collection provides item-level descriptions for a run of Indiana State Prison reels, AFC 2011/009: SR0760–SR0769. The entries describe 7-inch analogue sound-tape reels, including numbered reels #31–#40, with contents such as Blue Grass, sociological recordings, prison-condition discussions, blues and jazz. Several tape-box labels are explicitly described as coming from the Archives of Folk and Primitive Music, Indiana University. This is additional archival evidence for surviving reel objects associated with the 1962 Indiana prison project, but it does not by itself establish that every LOC item is an original IU accession or map each SR item to accession numbers 62-4-28 / 62-6-18 / 62-8-9.

This strengthens the existence and item-level content evidence for IM-013 while leaving the IU accession-to-LOC item concordance open. The 1962 IU series, LOC Bruce Jackson/Christian reels, Smithsonian 1963 reels and Smithsonian FW-ASCH-7RR-1966 remain separate archival records pending provenance reconciliation.
