## Recording and institutional identity

Harmonia Enlouquece is a music group formed at the Centro Psiquiátrico do Rio de Janeiro (CPRJ) on 7 April 2001. The official group site describes five albums and 66 recorded songs; the 15 August 2026 Agência Brasil report gives 65 recorded songs and says Hamilton de Jesus Assunção composed 62. Preserve the 65/66 discrepancy rather than silently updating the corpus count.

The 8 April 2026 Rio de Janeiro State Health Secretariat article describes the fifth album, then in preparation, as **O Quinto dos Infernos**. It identifies **“O Amor Chegou”** as a forthcoming highlight composed by Hamilton Assunção. The 15 August 2026 Agência Brasil report (syndicated by Linha Geral) reports the public launch at the Museu da História e da Cultura Afro-Brasileira (MuhCab), 13 tracks, 11 previously unreleased and authored by Assunção. The source does not attribute the other two tracks.

## Exact platform item

Amazon Music lists a 13-track album under the variant platform title **Esse 5° É Dos Infernos**, with a platform date of **5 July 2026**, total duration 46 minutes, and ℗© Hamilton Assunção. The 15 August public launch event and 5 July platform date are different dated events/metadata fields, not necessarily a conflict. Treat the platform title as a metadata variant of the officially announced event title **O Quinto dos Infernos** unless a physical sleeve or artist statement proves otherwise.

## Discrepancies and open questions

1. The April 2026 state article names “O Amor Chegou” as a highlight of the forthcoming fifth album, but that exact title is absent from the July 5 Amazon tracklist. It may have been omitted, renamed, issued separately, or described imprecisely; no mapping is established.
2. Apple Music's accessible artist page still exposes only the 2022 album *O quarto do Harmonia*. It lists a 2022 track “Hoje o Amor Chegou”; do not conflate this with the April 2026 “O Amor Chegou” announcement.
3. The official group site reports 66 songs; the 15 August Agência Brasil report reports 65. No reconciled master catalogue was found.
4. The press report attributes 11 of the 13 new album tracks to Hamilton Assunção; the other two tracks' authorship is not specified there. Do not assume all 13 are participant-composed or collectively authored.
5. The platform page is a public streaming/item page, not a direct audio-file or authorised download URL. ℗© metadata is not a reuse licence.

## Evidence and editorial decision

- **Institutional setting:** verified at CPRJ.
- **Recording/release identity:** platform item and 13-track list identified; official event title/date corroborated by the state-health and Agência Brasil reporting.
- **Participant authorship:** partial; 11 of 13 new tracks are attributed to Hamilton by the 15 August report.
- **Obscurity/discovery value:** remains low relative to the project's marginal-recording priority; this is a documented baseline/context record, not a new qualifying addition.
- **Access:** Amazon Music streaming page only; no direct download route found.
- **Rights:** ℗© platform metadata is not clearance. No permissions or reuse rights checked.

**Decision:** retain IM-039 as a context-only baseline lead. No new candidate, no Grok request, no TRACK_MAPPING change, no download or upload.
