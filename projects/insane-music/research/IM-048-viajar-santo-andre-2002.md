# IM-048 — *Viajar – Ida e Volta no Caminho do Inconsciente Poético e Musical* (Santo André, Brazil, 2002)

**Research status:** RESEARCHED-OPEN (2026-11-18)  
**Qualification:** qualified new lead for the main discography; community mental-health workshop setting, participant-authored lyrics and a mixed resident/volunteer performance model.  
**Object count:** one nine-track CD. The nine tracks are not nine independent leads.

## Environment and recording identity

A contemporary report in *Diário do Grande ABC* dated **13 January 2002** documents a CD made by users of Santo André’s psychosocial-care network with support from the municipal Health Secretariat. It names the CD as *Viajar – Ida e Volta no Caminho do Inconsciente Poético e Musical*, says it contains nine tracks with lyrics written by workshop students during 1999, and explains that regional artists volunteered new arrangements and selected songs to interpret.

The report names the following track/performance pairings:

- “Coração de Pedra” — Vasco Faé
- “Sopa de Sapato” — Fegato
- “Cadeiras e Cadeias” — Renê de França
- “Meu Sol” — Mônica Marsola
- “Vem Menino” — Kléber Albuquerque
- “Retirante” — Fábio Vieira
- “Qual é a Sua Mau Elemento” — Uafro
- “Viajar” — Jefferson Sooma and the CAPS-DQ choir
- “Vento Esperando” — listed with K.Ram.K.

The article describes the lyrics as written by workshop participants, but the arrangements and most named performances are by volunteer regional musicians. The title track “Viajar” explicitly includes the CAPS-DQ choir. This is therefore a **mixed-authorship and mixed-performance project**: it is not accurate to label all nine tracks as resident-performed recordings. It is, however, a distinct extant CD made within a community psychosocial-care music workshop, with participant-authored material and a documented service-user choir performance.

The contemporary report says the CDs were distributed to entities registered with the Secretariat and that other associations could contact the Secretariat to obtain the disc. This is direct contemporary evidence of a CD release and circulation in 2002; it does not establish current custody or a present-day copy.

## Methodological fit and limits

The lead fits the project’s community mental-health setting and workshop-authorship pathway. It is admitted as one CD-level lead, with the distinction between participant authorship and visiting/volunteer performance preserved. It is not described as an institutional recording of nine resident performances, nor is it classified through diagnosis or sensational framing.

The report does not identify the CD label, producer, recording studio, exact recording dates, master carrier, complete credit/rights chain or current holding institution. No physical CD or authorized digital transfer was located in this pass. No audio was auditioned. The CD’s existence is historically documented, but present-day access remains open.

## Provenance, access and rights

- **Creation context:** music workshops within Santo André’s psychosocial-care network; municipal Health Secretariat support.
- **Lyrics:** workshop participants, written over 1999 according to the 2002 report.
- **Arrangements/performance:** volunteer regional musicians; “Viajar” includes Jefferson Sooma and the CAPS-DQ choir.
- **Release/circulation:** nine-track CD documented on 13 January 2002; copies distributed to Secretariat-registered entities.
- **Current custody:** unknown; no current copy or institutional catalogue record located.
- **Rights:** lyric authorship belongs to workshop participants as reported, but individual authors, consent, publishing, master ownership and reuse permissions are not documented. No rights clearance is implied.
