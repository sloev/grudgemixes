# IM-049 — Tardes Nuestras opening theme, Rosario, Argentina

**Project:** Insane Music  
**Status:** IN PROGRESS — qualified new lead (2026-11-18)  
**Working title only:** “Dicen que no tenemos razón” (opening lyric; not a verified official title)

## Environment

*Tardes Nuestras* is a community-radio programme created in 2005 by users and workers associated with the Hospital Agudo Ávila and Centro Cultural Gomecito in Rosario, Santa Fe, Argentina. It is broadcast by FM Aire Libre. The 2020 *La Capital* report describes the programme as a collective project and records that it airs weekly on Wednesdays at 14:00.

## Recording lead

A 2017 Universidad Nacional de Rosario thesis records that the programme made contact with musicians and produced a song to serve as the *cortina* (opening/closing theme) of *Tardes Nuestras*. A 2020 *La Capital* article describes the recurring opening as a catchy rock song, quotes its opening lyric “Dicen que no tenemos razón,” and says that the song starts the programme. The station's official programme page links to its recorded-programme archive on RadioCut.

This is a specific music recording associated with a documented community-radio environment, rather than a generic mention of music on air. The working object is **one recurring theme embedded in recorded programme audio**, not every episode or every song mentioned by a presenter.

## Maker / performance classification

The 2017 thesis says the theme was produced with musicians, but does not identify them or establish that programme participants composed or performed it. Therefore classify this as **external-musician collaboration / broadcast playback in a community-radio environment**. Do not describe it as resident-made. The possibility of participant co-writing or performance remains open, not assumed.
