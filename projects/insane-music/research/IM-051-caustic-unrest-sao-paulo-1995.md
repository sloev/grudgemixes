# IM-051 — Caustic, *Unrest* (São Paulo, September 1995)

**Status:** PARTIALLY VERIFIED / PROMISING.  
**Pathway:** underground DIY / cassette circulation / independent electronic-industrial scene.  
**Count:** one eight-track cassette recording object, not eight track-level leads.

## Identity and production

Demo-Tapes Brasil's item-level post identifies Caustic's *Unrest* as a 1995 São Paulo cassette. It reports that the recording and mix were made by PB and Caustic in September 1995; the named formation is Jean Grimard (guitar and vocals), Lourenço “Loop B” (machines) and Fabrício (drums). The post supplies an eight-track sequence and a reported duration of 31:21, alongside nine images. It says the audio conversion and image digitisation were supplied by Dalton Silva.

The track sequence is: “Discharge the Hate”; “The party of poors”; “Steel in bone”; “Go on”; “Down and out”; “Copyright”; “Steel in bone - Primal Violence I”; and “Steel in bone - Primal Violence Remix II”. These titles are source metadata only; no lyrical, sonic or thematic interpretation is made here.

A separate Demo-Tapes Brasil post describes a three-track Caustic *Demo-tape* (1995), recorded in a home studio and distributed by Totem Records. It is a distinct named item and tracklist, but it is not merged into IM-051. It may warrant a separate future record after its relationship to *Unrest* is clarified.
