# IM-038 — Mágicos do Som: two distinct *Saúde Musical* CDs

**Project:** Insane Music  
**Baseline status:** RESEARCHED-OPEN (2026-11-18)  
**Counting rule:** one group-level baseline lead, with two distinct CD objects; do not count the two same-titled releases as separate new leads.

## Identity and chronology

Mágicos do Som is a music group associated with the CAPS Usina de Sonhos in Volta Redonda, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. The 2007 UFF thesis by Raquel Siqueira da Silva documents the group's work in the mental-health service context and identifies Regina Lúcia Serrão as composer and singer of “Preconceito” (2000). The thesis reproduces a 1999 clipping showing that the CD was still being prepared, and a November 2000 clipping reporting a launch on the 22nd. The extracted copy does not make the exact event date fully unambiguous, so the first *Saúde Musical* CD is dated **c. 2000**, not assigned a precise day.

The 2002 *Voices Resources* article independently calls the first CD *Saúde Musical* and presents “Preconceito” as track 2. Its historical MP3 link did not resolve in this research pass. The thesis says audio was included on an annex CD-ROM; that physical annex has not been accessed.

A municipal health-department notice dated 12 November 2013 announces the group's **second** CD launch for 13 November 2013, again titled *Saúde Musical*, with ten songs. It names six examples: “Na hora da virada,” “O bem e o mal,” “Mágicos do Som,” “Cantar no Mágicos do Som,” “As quatro estações,” and “O que eu quero.” A 2021 municipal retrospective independently says the group had released two CDs and identifies 2013 as the latest release.

**Resolution:** the title collision is best explained by two distinct releases reusing *Saúde Musical*: a first CD around 2000 and a second CD in 2013. This is not evidence that one disc was reissued under a new date. Keep both release objects distinct inside the single IM-038 group-level case.

## Environment and music-making

The thesis and municipal notice place the group in a community mental-health service setting. The first CD includes “Preconceito,” written and sung by group member Regina Lúcia Serrão. The 2013 notice describes ten songs authored by group members, with volunteer musicians providing accompaniment. These are resident/participant-associated recordings, not outside media merely played in the setting.

## Access, provenance and rights

- Second CD: the 2013 notice confirms a launch and ten-song programme, but no current authorised stream, complete sleeve scan or physical copy was verified.

## 2026-11-19 source expansion — participant-authored anti-stigma protest song

A direct re-check of Raquel Siqueira da Silva's 2007 UFF dissertation (*Cartografias de uma experimentação musical: entre a musicoterapia e o grupo Mágicos do Som*) confirms that “Preconceito” is a composition by group member Regina Lúcia Serrão (2000), and that the thesis's annex CD-ROM includes the first *Saúde Musical* CD. The dissertation frames the group's music-making as collective participation and a public challenge to stigma; it does not make the recording a clinical artefact. The annex CD-ROM remains unaccessed, so this is an item-level recording reference, not an audition or transfer verification.

A 2016 peer-reviewed *Sociologias* article (18/43, pp. 208–237; DOI 10.1590/15174522-018004308) independently identifies “Preconceito” as a composition by Lúcia Serrão performed by Mágicos do Som. Its analysis reads the song as a collective anti-discrimination statement, connecting mental-health-service users' experience with broader prejudice and segregation. This is the strongest new interpretive evidence: the song is not merely an incidental track on a therapy CD, but a participant-authored protest statement. No lyrics are reproduced here; the source's interpretation is paraphrased.

The 12 November 2013 Volta Redonda municipal notice gives a second, distinct *Saúde Musical* CD with ten tracks, six named titles, and says the compositions were authored by the group. It names seven members and separately names coordinator Isaías das Neves Silva and two musicians at the launch event (Auriston on keyboard, Carlos on bass); do not assume the launch performers are the exact recording lineup. The municipal notice dates the group's origin to March 1996, while the 2016 scholarly article says it was formed in 1997. Preserve this as a 1996/1997 chronology conflict until an original programme or contemporaneous record resolves it.

**Research effect:** RESEARCHED-OPEN remains appropriate. The two CD objects remain distinct within one group-level lead. The first CD's c.2000 chronology and “Preconceito” authorship are strengthened; the 2013 CD's group authorship and named event personnel are strengthened. Still unresolved: exact formation date, complete 10-track list, recording (not launch) personnel, both carrier/sleeve/catalogue IDs, authorized audio access and rights. No audio was played, no CD-ROM or physical disc inspected, and no contact or rights request made.
