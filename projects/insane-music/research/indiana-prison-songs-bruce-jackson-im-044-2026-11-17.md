# IM-044 — Bruce Jackson, Indiana Prison Songs (reels 1–2), 1963

**Status:** PARTIALLY VERIFIED — two exact archival reel records and tracklists located; prison facility, session detail and access copy provenance need clarification  
**Visibility:** PROMISING / high research value as an unissued or less-circulated reel corpus  
**Pathway:** G — prison recording environment  
**Medium:** Two 7-inch analogue open-reel recordings; Smithsonian catalogue also notes CDR copies

## Identification

The Smithsonian Institution's Ralph Rinzler Folklife Archives and Collections catalogue identifies two items titled “Bruce Jackson Field Recordings: Indiana Prison Songs” in the Moses and Frances Asch Collection:

- **Reel 1:** FW-ASCH-7RR-4919; Smithsonian item CFCH.ASCH; GUID https://n2t.net/ark:/65665/bk51ff9a544-1229-4eb5-ab1e-5418207aaad3
- **Reel 2:** FW-ASCH-7RR-4918; Smithsonian item CFCH.ASCH; GUID https://n2t.net/ark:/65665/bk5bb7820af-d899-4cf3-863b-0604acb02479

The catalogue describes each as a 7-inch analogue sound-tape reel. Reel 1 has an event note “Recorded in: United States, Indiana, 1963.” Both entries identify Bruce Jackson as recorder and Eugene Rhodes and Hank Ferguson among the performers. The archive's records say the Eugene Rhodes and Hank Ferguson material was released by Folk Legacy Records, but do not specify which tracks or edition in the visible record.

## Track contents as catalogued

**Reel 1 (FW-ASCH-7RR-4919):**
- Eugene Rhodes: “Who went out the back?”, “Talkin' about my time”, “Prison cell blues”, “If you see my savior”, “I just keep wonderin'”
- Hank Ferguson: “The wall”, “Life”, “I'm not living”, “How Hank got in prison”, “Goin' Down the road feeling bad”
- Additional listed items/performers: “Strawberry”, “Red wing”, “John Henry” (the catalogue's punctuation does not make the performer attribution fully clear)

**Reel 2 (FW-ASCH-7RR-4918):**
- Hank Ferguson: “Charming Betsy”, “Wished I stayed in the wagon yard”, “Gotta travel on”, “Wreck of the old #9”, “Wreck of the old 97”, “Waiting on a train”
- Billy Turpin: “The Walls”
- Charlie Bell: “A convict's warning”
- Eugene Rhodes: “I don't want no woman”, “Backwater blues”, “Oh lordy mama”, “If you ever been to prison”, “Death cell blues”

The Smithsonian records classify the material under prisoners' songs and folk songs. The titles suggest a mixture of prison testimony, work/labour themes, blues, spirituals and general folk repertoire; titles alone do not establish which songs were written by prisoners or their precise thematic stance.

## Relationship to existing IM-013

Do **not** merge these reels automatically with IM-013 (Bruce Jackson, Indiana State Prison tapes, 1962). The 1963 reels have distinct Smithsonian local numbers and named performer/track metadata. They may be an additional recording session or a related archive subset. The exact prison site and relationship to Jackson's 1962 Indiana State Prison accessions remain to be checked.
