# The *Unwanted* strand: annoying music, rough music and the music nobody asked for

**Added 2026-11-20 by Claude at the owner's request** ("expand with annoying music and weirdo music, make it fit"). **Status: proposal.** No track listened to; no rights checked beyond flags.

## How it fits

*Music in Spite* is music made where it was not expected or not tolerated. The *Unwanted* strand turns that around and asks who decides what is unwanted. It has three branches, each tied to the book's subject, which is the grudge as a social practice:

1. **Rough music.** Annoying noise as a community's verdict. Charivari, *cencerrada*, *scampanata*, *Katzenmusik*, skimmington and the Canadian "horse-fiddle" are rituals of deliberately awful sound outside an offender's house. This is the sung grudge's noisy cousin: a public, collective sentence passed without a court. Grudge Mixes can take it too if a pre-1926 recording is found.
2. **Music as nuisance.** Music used to annoy, repel or wear people down: songs engineered to be hated, music played to drive people away, the hold tune you cannot escape, the piece repeated 840 times. Here spite is the *use* of the music, not its maker.
3. **Unwanted voices ("weirdo" music).** People who made music the culture laughed at or ignored, and made it anyway: family bands, self-funded albums, paid-for song-poems, homemade instruments. This is where the strand meets the existing Insane Music pathways C–E (self-taught, DIY, outsider practice).

## Rules for this strand (in addition to `method.md`)

- **The joke is on taste, never on the makers.** Write about makers with the same care as everyone else in the series. Rejection is part of the story; ridicule is not our voice. A spitebites.party wink is allowed in the framing of branch 2 (deliberate nuisance), not branch 3.
- **Strangeness is not the admission ticket** (`method.md` §4). Admit only where the spite relationship is documented: who rejected the music, who used it against whom, or what ridicule the maker faced.
- **Famous anchors are context.** By the obscurity rule (§3B), well-known items (The Shaggs, Portsmouth Sinfonia, "The Most Unwanted Song", "Vexations") are *CONTEXT ONLY / anchor* and may open or close a volume at most. Discovery effort goes to obscure items: archive tapes of rough music, individual song-poem 45s, local eccentrics outside the US and UK.
- **Privacy.** Rough-music recordings target real, often private, people (traditionally widows and widowers who remarried). Use only where nobody targeted can be identified, or where the target is a public authority (modern political *caceroladas*).
- **No military or prison "music torture".** It belongs to the confinement strand the owner asked us to limit. Context only.
