## Waupun / Upheaval — verified surviving track and video routes (2026-11-15)

**Band and environment:** Upheaval was a nine-piece soul band formed and developed within Wisconsin State Prison / Waupun Correctional Institution. The strongest account is Max Wilson's 2022 investigation, based on interviews with Joe Hayes and material from the Wisconsin Historical Society. The group composed and performed inside the prison music programme; do not treat it as a merely prison-themed outside act.

**Additional unreleased-corpus leads, not yet directly accessible:** Joe Hayes reportedly held a CD with “Vampire Lady” and other Upheaval recordings; LaVelle Rudd's reel-to-reels and music tapes were reportedly bequeathed to his girlfriend, whereabouts unverified. Member-circulated cassettes were described as degraded and unsuitable for reissue. These are oral-history leads, not verified extant masters or a complete tracklist.

**Important date discrepancy:** the original single “Paradise Lost” / “Now That You're Gone” is dated 26 Aug 1974 in Max Wilson's article. Symphonical's “Come On with the Come On” / “TLC” double-sider is described by specialist retailers as recorded inside Waupun in 1979, whereas Wilson's article caption calls the tracks circa 1974. Preserve the disagreement; do not force a single date without primary carrier evidence. Retailer/catalogue sources identify Symphonical catalogue SYM001 / SR001, released 18 Feb 2016, with two tracks and the claim that surviving masters were cassette recordings.

**Research classification:** released 45 and later reissues are now well-covered among specialist soul collectors: CONTEXT ONLY for the known singles. The 1975 rehearsal footage and any unissued Upheaval repertoire are stronger HIGH PRIORITY targets, but the footage is a visual/audio-video archive item, not a standalone music release. Do not describe it as unknown; its rediscovery has been covered in 2022 and archival screening in 2024.
