# Insane Music

A worldwide discography and archival map of overlooked music made in psychiatric hospitals, prisons, camps, Hansen's-disease settlements, closed communities and other marginal environments, and by self-taught, DIY and outsider musicians.

*Working public title under consideration: **Music in Spite**: recordings from the margins, made in spite of it all.* The repository title is deliberately provocative. It is not a clinical judgement, and the project's own prose does not use it as a label for people. See [method.md](method.md) §1 and §8.

> **We collect the music, not the diagnoses.**

## Highest-priority research direction

A genuinely global, non-Anglo perspective takes priority over convenience or the inherited queue. Search local languages and community sources across Africa, Asia, Latin America and the Caribbean, the Pacific, the Middle East, and eastern/southeastern Europe. Treat the small/smol web—old personal sites, blogs, forums, webrings, blogrolls, cassette pages and archived homepages—as core discovery infrastructure. Preserve original scripts and terminology; English visibility is not a measure of importance.
