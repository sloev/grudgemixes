# GM-NG-001 — «Iquo Ekong» / «Iquo Ubom» (Ibibio / Efik Odeon shellac pair)

**Status:** C — LEAD.  
**Core eligibility:** UNRESOLVED — the archive says “1920s”, which does not establish a pre-1926-01-01 fixation date.  
**Thematic fit:** UNVERIFIED — a Cambridge collection description classifies both sides as “society songs” in the genre of war songs, but no text, translation or performance narrative was inspected. Do not infer revenge, grievance or retaliation from the genre label alone.

## Exact recording object and crosswalk

The University of Cambridge World Oral Literature Project hosts **Daniel Morgan: Ibibio Odeon shellac recordings**. Its collection page describes five 78rpm shellac records with two songs per disc (ten tracks), in Efik, believed to have been recorded in south-eastern Nigeria during the 1920s. The collection note says the recordings were given to George Kearley Weeks by Odeon-Electric and deposited online by Daniel Morgan.

The fifth disc is listed as:
- **Side A:** «Iquo Ekong»
- **Side B:** «Iquo Ubom»
- Cambridge collection description: both are “society songs” in the genre of war songs; no translation or text is supplied on the collection page.

A specialist West African Odeon discography supplies a **probable, not yet physically verified, discographic crosswalk**:
- **Odeon A 248026**
- Performer credit as transcribed by the discography: **Sambouwa Udö Udö Uwa Eya Owut Ibaña and Choir**
- A: **IQUO EKONG** (Ibibio)
- B: **IQUO UBOM** (Ibibio)

The matching pair of titles strongly suggests that the Cambridge fifth disc and Odeon A 248026 are the same issue, but no original label, sleeve, matrix/runout or accession record was inspected to prove the match. Preserve the original spelling and the discography's diacritics; do not normalise the performer name without label evidence.
