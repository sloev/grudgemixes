# GM-ES-001 — María García, “La noche de Reyes” (Asturias; recorded 19 January 1939)

**Status:** OUTSIDE CORE — verified archival field recording; contextual lead only  
**Proposed role:** domestic violence, accusation and retaliatory killing in an Asturian ballad  
**Recording identity:** Library of Congress AFS 3844b2–b3; 78 rpm field disc

## What is documented

The Library of Congress item record identifies María García as performer and Sidney Robertson Cowell as collector. The song was recorded in Monterey, California, on 19 January 1939, as part of the WPA California Folk Music Project. The performance is unaccompanied, in Spanish, and occupies two bands of a 12-inch 78 rpm disc. The LOC subject headings explicitly include “Revenge—Songs and music.”

The LOC's own dust-jacket note explains the narrative: a father tells his young son that he found the child's mother with her lover and killed her. This is a narrative of retaliatory violence framed as a ballad, not evidence that the performer endorses the killing. The full item record lists two bands, AFS 3844b2 and AFS 3844b3, and a digital audio object.

**Primary source:** https://www.loc.gov/item/2017700908/  
**Digital object:** https://hdl.loc.gov/loc.afc/afc1940001.afc1940001_a3844

## Date and eligibility

The recording is securely dated 1939 and therefore **outside the project's pre-1926 core**. Retain it as a contextual comparison and a potential route to earlier versions of the ballad, not as a core candidate. The Asturian provenance describes the song tradition and the singer's repertory context; it does not establish that the ballad originated in Asturias.

## Thematic reading

The documented plot is a killing in response to an alleged sexual betrayal. Before classifying the work as revenge, distinguish:
- the father's narrated motive;
- the child's position as listener;
- the ballad's narrative stance;
- the performer's own stance, which is not established by the catalogue.

Do not reproduce the violence as a sensational anecdote. The archive description supports the story outline but does not substitute for a complete transcription or specialist interpretation of the Spanish text.

## Decision

A strong, exact, publicly catalogued revenge-ballad recording, but **not eligible for the pre-1926 core**. Its value is as a post-cutoff comparison and as a pointer toward earlier Iberian ballad witnesses. No listening or transcription was performed in this pass.
