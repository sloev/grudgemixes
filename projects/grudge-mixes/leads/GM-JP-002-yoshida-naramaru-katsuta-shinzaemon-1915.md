# GM-JP-002 — 吉田奈良丸, “勝田新左エ門” (Victor 50181–50182; catalogue year 1915)

**Status:** C — qualified lead; exact recording text and fixation date unresolved.  
**Project fit:** possible Akō vendetta / retaliatory justice narrative.  
**Core eligibility:** NOT ESTABLISHED. The specialist discography gives 1915 in its year field, but this is not proof of recording/session date. Do not promote to the pre-1926 recording core until fixation date is independently established.

## Recording-family identity

A specialist Japanese Victor SP discography lists 吉田奈良丸 (Yoshida Naramaru) performing “勝田新左エ門” in four parts, assigned to catalogue sides 50181-A1, 50181-B2, 50182-A3 and 50182-B3, with 1915 in the catalogue year field and 浪花節 (rōkyoku) as the genre. Treat all four sides as one narrative recording family, not four leads. The catalogue's romanisation/character form should be retained as printed; do not silently normalise the historical title.

The Japan Arts Council's popular-entertainment history confirms that the second Yoshida Naramaru was a prominent early-recording-period rōkyoku performer and that his performances were issued on SP records and in shorthand/stenographic books. It supplies useful medium/context but does not identify this specific recording's session or text.

## Historical referent and thematic rationale

Kotobank's entry in the *Nihon Daihyakka Zensho* identifies 勝田新左衛門武堯 (Katsuta Shinzaemon Taketaka, 1680–1703) as an Akō retainer and one of the forty-seven rōnin who attacked Kira's residence on 14 December 1702 and were ordered to commit seppuku on 4 February 1703. This establishes that the named person belongs to the Akō vendetta tradition; it is not proof that the recording narrates the raid, nor that it expresses the same moral interpretation as later Chūshingura works.

The National Diet Library's reference case also records the historical figure's assumed merchant identity 嘉右衛門 while gathering information about Kira's residence. This is contextual evidence only. No performance text or contemporary synopsis for the Victor sides has yet been found.
