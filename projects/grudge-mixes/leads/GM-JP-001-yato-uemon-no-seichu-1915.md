# GM-JP-001 — 吉田奈良丸, “矢頭右衛門の誠忠” (Victor catalogue, 1915)

**Status:** C — LEAD. Counted as one qualified new lead, not as a verified track.

## Recording identity

The specialist Victor SP catalogue lists a four-side sequence under 1915:

- 50188 A/B and 50189 A/B
- Performer: 吉田奈良丸
- Title: 矢頭右衛門の誠忠
- Repeated episode annotation: 赤穂城内大評定

Count the four sides as one narrative recording family, not four leads. The catalogue year is treated as a publication/issue year; it does not establish the fixation date. No matrix/runout number or primary label scan was located in this pass.

## Thematic fit and limits

A 2015 Kobecco historical feature places 矢頭右衛門七 in the Ako-retainer revenge tradition: his father joined the revenge pledge, and the son is represented as taking up the cause after his father's death. This is useful secondary narrative context for the title’s “loyal fidelity” framing, but it does not establish the exact text performed on these four sides.

The catalogue’s 赤穂城内大評定 annotation is treated as a performance/story label, not as a verified historical event. The actual recorded wording and whether the four sides narrate the council, the later revenge, or a conventionalized Chūshingura episode remain unknown.

The 1915 performer credit is 吉田奈良丸. Kotobank’s biography of the second Yoshida Naramaru (1880–1967) and his reputation for 義士銘々伝 make the second generation plausible for this date, but the catalogue does not state the generation. Keep the generation attribution provisional.
