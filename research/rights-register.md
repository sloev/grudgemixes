## How to read the US sound-recording column

Under 17 U.S.C. §1401 (Music Modernization Act, 2018), pre-1972 sound recordings first published **before 1923** left protection on 1 Jan 2022. Those first published **1923–1946** are protected until the end of the 100th year after first publication. “First published” means publication in the US or anywhere else; it is not the recording date. Several sources in this repo give only a recording date. Do not substitute one for the other (METHODOLOGY §1).

## Rules carried forward

- A third-party mirror's “Public domain” statement (e.g. the Hessel Digital Library notes on archive.org) is **that uploader's assertion**, not evidence. Cite the statute and the publication date instead.
- Public domain in the US says nothing about the UK, the EU or Japan.
- A restoration or remaster on a modern reissue (Archeophone, Jazzman-style label reissues, CHARM, LOC) can carry its own contractual or neighbouring-right claims. Record who made each file.
