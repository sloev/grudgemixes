# The wider universe of *Music in Spite*

Insane Music's admission rule (an extant, identifiable recording with documented context) and ethics in `projects/insane-music/method.md` still govern every entry.

## Definition

*Music in Spite* is music made where it was not expected or not tolerated, by people who paid a price to make it anyway. The book defines spite as accepting a cost to yourself in order to impose one on someone else. Here the cost is turned around: the maker pays it, and what they deny the other side is silence.

## Found tapes

- Home tapes, demos, answering-machine cassettes and private-press projects turn up in any thrift store (https://anomalyindex.com/tag/found-sound/). The Cassette Archive uploads tapes with no online footprint (https://anomalyindex.com/tag/adimus/). Intertapes crowdsources scans, audio and find stories (https://www.webpronews.com/intertapes-crowdsourced-archive-revives-forgotten-cassette-tapes/).
- Already catalogued: IM-043 "A FITA" (found in a wall, Rio); IM-046 Ata Kak (copy found in Cape Coast); IM-040/041/050 Brazilian demo tapes.
- **Proposed rule:** a found tape is a lead until a tracing attempt is documented. Publish only with the finder's provenance story, a public "is this yours?" notice, and royalties held for the maker. Exclude private speech (diaries, voicemails, therapy or medical tapes).
