# Diversity pass 2026-11-20 — beyond prisons

**Status: LEADS.** Owner direction: prison and institution material dominates; find diverse sources and makers. Each lead has a story, the exact recording where known, and the best public access found. "Direct file" is given only where a file URL was actually seen; otherwise the listening page.

## Notes

- Items 1, 2, 3, 5 and 7 are pre-1926 and could strengthen Grudge Mixes' global reach; items 4 and 6 extend *Music in Spite* to women's voices in Iran and Algeria.
- Rights: 1902–1917 recordings are out of protection as recordings; compositions to check (Donga d. 1974 and Mauro de Almeida, so "Pelo Telefone" composition is protected in the EU until 2044 — ⚠). Rimitti estate for item 6. Archive terms for 3 and 7.
