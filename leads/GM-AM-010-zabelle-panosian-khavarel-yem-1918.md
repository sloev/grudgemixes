## Thematic assessment

The Canary Records title gloss is “I Am Darkened.” Armenian lexical sources confirm that *խաւարել / խավարել* can mean to darken, become gloomy, or be overwhelmed by sorrow, but this is only a lexical clue, not a translation of the song. The Panosian project includes translated song texts and historical notes, but the full lyric of this item has not yet been independently checked in this research pass.

Panosian's wider career was deeply connected to Armenian diaspora relief and public remembrance after the destruction of her hometown Bardizag in 1915. Her benefit concerts raised money for survivors, and the Armenian-American press framed her as a voice of a bereaved community. That context may be relevant to reception, but it must not be used to infer that this particular song contains a grievance, accusation, refusal, or retaliatory response.

**Current taxonomy:** unknown; possible lament/context only. If the text is solely mournful, it remains outside the core thematic selection. If it contains a demand, accusation, vow, refusal, or resistance, reassess using the exact 1918 performance and translation.
