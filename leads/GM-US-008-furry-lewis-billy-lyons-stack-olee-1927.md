# Furry Lewis — “Billy Lyons and Stack O'Lee” (Vocalion 1132, 1927)

**Project ID:** GM-US-008  
**Status:** C — LEAD / exact session, matrices and issue identified; lyrics not yet transcribed from the master  
**Session:** Chicago, 9 October 1927  
**Matrices:** C-1244 / C-1245  
**Issue:** Vocalion 1132; later Brunswick 80092  
**Performer:** Furry Lewis, vocal and guitar.

## Identity and source evidence

Alan B. Govenar's / Folkways historical reissue notes list “Billy Lyons and Stack O'Lee,” Vocalion 1132, as part of the October 1927 Chicago session. https://folkways-media.si.edu/docs/folkways/artwork/FW03823.pdf

The illustrated Walter “Furry” Lewis discography identifies the title as C-1244/45, recorded 9 October 1927 in Chicago, and issued on Vocalion 1132 (US, February 2, 1928); Brunswick 80092 reissued it under the title “Stackerlee.” https://www.wirz.de/music/lewis.htm

A specialist commercial discography independently gives the full session/matrix sequence and exact date. https://www.grooves-inc.co.uk/furry-lewis-furrys-blues-1927-1929-document-cd-pZZa1-1899420523.html

## Thematic assessment

The title explicitly names Billy Lyons and Stack O'Lee, the two figures at the centre of the Stagger Lee ballad cycle. This is a strong research lead for a song about retaliatory violence, but the exact lyric and moral point of view must be established from this performance. Later summaries and familiar lyrics are not substitutes for the 1927 master.

Compare this performance with Ford and Ford's 1924 “Skeeg-A-Lee Blues,” the rare 1927 Reed/Hull “Original Stack O'Lee Blues,” and Mississippi John Hurt's 1928 “Stack O'Lee.” These are distinct performances and potentially distinct narrative variants.
