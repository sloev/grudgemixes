## Exact recording identity

- Performer credit: Al Craver (Columbia pseudonym for Vernon Dalhart)
- Title: “Sentenced to Life Behind These Grey Walls” (catalogue spelling)
- Label / catalogue: Columbia 15060-D, side 2
- Matrix: W141311
- Recording date: 24 November 1925 (secondary matrix listing); Smithsonian NMAH independently states side 2 was recorded in 1925.
- Paired side: “The Engineer's Dying Child,” side 1; Smithsonian explicitly dates that other side to 1926. Do not transfer side 1's 1926 date to this side.
- Smithsonian NMAH object 1996.0320.14128: https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object/nmah_1291936
- Matrix/date listing: https://www.dm4ep.com/music/yr1925.htm

## Thematic assessment

The full Columbia title explicitly frames the subject as a life sentence and confinement. This is a strong title-level punishment lead, but the title alone does not tell us whether the singer accepts the sentence, protests wrongful conviction, laments separation, or speaks in another dramatic register. No verified lyric transcription has yet been found for matrix W141311.

Do not conflate it with the separate Tobe Little Okeh 40532 performance, matrix 73826-A, also titled “Behind These Grey Walls,” or with other later label issues. Similar title and common performer are not proof of master identity. The 1925 Columbia matrix W141311 is the retrieval anchor for this dossier.
