# GM-AL-001 — Shoqnia e hengxhive e shahirave, «Kanga e Oso Kukës» (Gramophone 10771; matrix 4570r; Shkodër, 1908)

**Status:** C — qualified lead; exact 1908 performance text not yet compared with the later song text.  
**Date assessment:** PROBABLE DATE — the Shkodër session is dated approximately 4–10 May 1908 by the 2024 discographic study, based on Max Hampe's itinerary.  
**Project fit:** historical defiance / armed resistance narrative.  
**Count:** one single recording object; not a count for the wider 1908 Shkodër session or for the related «Kanga e Ali Pashës».

## Recording identity and provenance

Edmir Ballgjati's 2024 article in *Shejzat* reconstructs The Gramophone Company's 1908 Shkodër expedition by cross-checking the Oso Kuka Historical Museum disc collection, the company's matrix list and a Budapest branch catalogue. Its table identifies matrix **4570r**, performer **Shoqnia e hengxhive e shahirave**, title **Kanga e Oso Kukës**, catalogue/issue number **10771**. The note records the spelling visible on the disc as **“Chenga e Oso Kuks.”** Retain both the catalogue transcription and the disc-label spelling; do not silently normalise the historical label.

The study estimates that the Shkodër sessions took place around 4–10 May 1908. This is an itinerary-based probable range, not a separately documented day/time for this matrix. The physical disc was consulted by the article's author in the Historical Museum “Oso Kuka” in Shkodër; this research pass did not inspect the disc or its label.

## Important archival breakthrough: a digitised reel reference

Ballgjati's footnote 120 lists the 11 Shkodër-expedition matrices that were transferred to magnetic tape and are now digitised in the Audiovisual Archive of IAKSA. **4570r is among them, with reel reference 0279-03.** The article also states that IAKSA's gramophone-disc fund does not hold an original disc from this 1908 expedition; the original 4570r disc is instead associated with the Oso Kuka Museum collection. This is a precise, source-backed archival route to an extant transfer, not proof that the current project has access to it. No audio was played, downloaded or inspected.

## The historical narrative and why it qualifies

A 2023 Albanian-language cultural article published by the Albanian Ministry of Defence describes a song about Oso Kuka, attributing its composition to **Zenel Maha** and dating its creation to the time of the 1862 Vranina battle. It says the song comprised 22 stanzas and was first performed by a Shkodër aheng ensemble in a local coffeehouse. The account presents Oso Kuka's stand against Montenegrin forces as a narrative of resistance and refusal to surrender. A scholarly study of Gjergj Fishta's *Lahuta e Malcis* independently situates the five-canto Oso Kuka cycle in 1862 and identifies it as a central episode of the Albanian national epic.

This is a meaningful defiance narrative, not a generic patriotic title. However, the 2023 article's 22-stanza song and the 1908 Gramophone performance have **not** been matched line-for-line. The Ministry article is a later retrospective account, not a contemporary lyric sheet or transcription of matrix 4570r. Therefore the thematic identification remains C-level and must not be promoted solely from the title.

- **Extant audio transfer:** WELL-SUPPORTED as a digitised IAKSA reel entry 0279-03, according to the 2024 article; not accessed in this pass.

- **Rights / access:** UNKNOWN. No access permission, duplication right or reuse licence is implied by the reel reference or museum catalogue.

## Deduplication and related objects

- Count **4570r / 10771** as one recording object.
- Do not merge it with the separate 1908 **«Kanga e Ali Pashës»** entry (matrix 4572r? / catalogue 10773); Ballgjati marks the matrix number as uncertain and that song requires its own thematic assessment.
- Do not merge it with the much later literary cycle in Fishta's *Lahuta e Malcis*; that is a contextual text witness.
- Do not count the 11 transferred reels as 11 new leads from this one item.
